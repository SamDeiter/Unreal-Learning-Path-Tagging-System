# Dark in Berlin': A Creative and Virtual Production Breakdown with the Filmmakers | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores how the makers of independent short film 'Dark in Berlin' used Unreal Engine to create dynamic and interactive environments for capture in an LED volume. 

With the goal of a photoreal portrayal of Berlin, this 15-minute film was made without any on-location shooting. Watch this session exploring its making, including its reliance on interactive elements between the virtual and physical worlds. 
This revealing case study examines the relationship between the production designer and VAD supervisor; explores the collaborative nature of world building; and demonstrates how virtual production and Unreal Engine can cut costs compared to filming on location.
