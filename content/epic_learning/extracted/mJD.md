# Animation Workflows in Unreal Engine 5.6 | A-COM Animation Sample

*This series with Sir Wade Neistadt provides an in-depth look at Unreal Engine 5.6’s animation tools using the A-COM animation sample. You will begin by exploring project structure, level sequences, cameras, and character rigs before diving into animation data workflows and keyframe editing.*


## Exploring the Animation Sample Production Files | A-COM Animation Sample | Lesson 1

Dive into professional cinematic production using Unreal Engine! This course uses a full sample project featuring stylized characters developed by Agora Studio to guide you through industry animation workflows.

You will learn to manage the project structure, including editing the pace of your cinematic shots and utilizing nested level sequences (sub-sequences) for organization. Explore environmental control by adjusting the master level and understanding how world assets (possessibles) interact with shot-specific elements (spawnables).

Gain crucial skills by adjusting camera setups, using dedicated control rigs, and optimizing your viewports with dual panels for efficient animation. Finally, get hands-on experience with the modular character rigs, practicing setup and exploring controls for IK/FK switching and fully rigged faces. Prepare to dig into the animation tools and bring these high-quality assets to life.

Start your journey by exploring the full Unreal Engine 5 project, developed by Agora Studio, which showcases a professional cinematic workflow, including an animatic and the final sequence. You will gain hands-on experience adjusting the editing and pace of the master level sequence clips while learning valuable tips about project organization and safe iteration practices

Master your environment by learning how the master level utilizes nested sub-levels (like lighting or damage) to enable efficient organizational control and support multi-artist production workflows. Crucially, you will understand the difference between possessible assets that exist in the world and spawnable assets (such as characters) that only appear for specific shots, giving you control over dynamic scene changes.

Learn advanced organizational techniques by dissecting sub-sequences (nested level sequences), which isolate data like animation, lighting, and effects within each shot, offering superior artistic control. Optimize your workflow by setting up dual viewport layouts to efficiently animate dedicated camera control rigs and adjust visual elements, such as the intensity of camera shake, in real-time.

Get hands-on with the modular character rigs created by Agora Studio, including the essential first step of setting a master key to prime crucial functions like IK/FK snapping. Discover how to manipulate professional controls, such as easily switching between IK and FK for the arms and legs, and utilizing the fully rigged facial controls to bring life and emotion to your stylized characters.
