# CLO & Epic Games: Bringing The Worlds of Fashion & Technology Closer Together | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which CLO explores how the convergence of fashion and technology is transforming industries, from high-street fashion brands to visual effects in gaming and film.

You’ll learn how CLO Virtual Fashion has been at the cutting edge of accurate garment simulation for over 20 years, driving innovation alongside household name brands, major studios and creatives from various disciplines.

There’s also a showcase of how the CLO-Epic partnership is yielding big advances for industry, as well as tips on how to take advantage of the latest collaborative projects between the two companies, such as Chaos Panel Cloth workflow and LiveSync for CLO and Marvelous Designer.
