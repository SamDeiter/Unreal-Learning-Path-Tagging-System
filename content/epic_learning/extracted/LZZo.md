# Chaos Cloth - Updates 5.6

*The Beta Chaos Cloth Panel Editor in 5.6 features our new Experimental Unified Dataflow Editor, the concept of an Outfit Asset with resizing/refitting capabilities and a host of other improvements including Cloth to Cloth constraints, Simulation Morph Target support and updates to various Cloth Dataflow nodes. This document will provide an overview of these 5.6 changes as we continue to expand and develop new capabilities for our Cloth Dataflow Editor.*
