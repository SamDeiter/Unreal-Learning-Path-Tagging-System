# Journey into Real-Time Animation | Unreal Fest 2023

*More and more animation studios are transitioning from their linear CG pipelines to nonlinear real-time pipelines leveraging Unreal Engine. Join our panel, which includes team members from Nickelodeon, Digital Dimension, and Steamroller Animation, as they share their first hand experience on the benefits and challenges of developing new tools and creative workflows with real-time technology.*
