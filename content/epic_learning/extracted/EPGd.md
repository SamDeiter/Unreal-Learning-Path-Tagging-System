# XR Streaming and Digital Twins: Enabling an Open Infrastructure Metaverse | Unreal Fest 2024

*The talk will demonstrate how Bentley's iTwin plugin works with Innoactive's  XR content management platform, guiding you through a simple workflow to produce highly performant, immersive experiences for your customers. Starting with streaming BIM and CAD data into Unreal Engine from various sources or design tools, followed by the creation of compelling and interactive content for uses within architectural visualisation, training, simulation and more. Then you will learn how to effortlessly deploy the scenarios to VR headsets or stream directly to a browser.*
