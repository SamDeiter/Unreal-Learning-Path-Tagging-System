# Qiddiya: Simulating the Saudi Giga-Project with Imerza | Unreal Fest 2023

*Join Imerza for an in-depth look at how the Saudi Giga-project utilizes Unreal Engine to help make critical decisions with their massive, 500-square-kilometer digital twin. Learn how Imerza is managing complex data pipelines and using the latest engine features like PCG to populate large-scale environments, and find out how they integrate intelligent traffic systems and GIS data to perform complex simulations and predictive analytics.*
