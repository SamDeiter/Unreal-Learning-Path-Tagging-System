# Amsterdam ZuidasDOK: A Digital Twin Platform Built on UE 5.4 | Unreal Fest 2024

*Behold the dynamic digital replica of Amsterdam's illustrious financial hub, intricately crafted to be navigated effortlessly through touch panel, mouse, or seamlessly managed remotely via web interface.

In this session, VERO Agency will leverage their experience with Amsterdam ZuidasDOK to discuss how to build in Unreal Engine at city scale without losing top-tier quality, speed, or performance. 

VERO will also look at the process of integrating different kinds of 3D sources into one unified masterplan experience; the seamless integration of dynamic data to enhance the depth and richness of your experience; and how to tailor the technology to suit a myriad of diverse cases and usage scenarios.*
