# Introduction to Motion Design

*"In this Unreal Fest lab, you'll learn to wield Unreal Engine's powerful new Motion Design toolset for Motion and Broadcast Graphics.

Learn to use Motion Design's Level Editor Mode, Procedural Geometry Tools, Material Designer, Cloners & Effectors, Rundown / Show Control and more. Designed for beginners and packed with content, this lab is equally valuable for Unreal Users that may be new to Motion Design, and Motion Designers that are new to Unreal.

By the end of this session, you'll have a solid foundation in Unreal's Motion Design Toolset."*
