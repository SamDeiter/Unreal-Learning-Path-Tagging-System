# Army of Ants: The Art and the Craft | GDC 2024

*Join Renaud Charpentier, Creative Director of Tower Five, as he unveils the innovative journey of crafting 'Empire of Ants,' a captivating cinematic experience from an artisan game studio. Delve into the intersection of creativity and technology as Charpentier discusses the transformative power of Unreal Engine 5 in shaping immersive storytelling. Discover how game technology paves the way for interactive cinema and redefines the boundaries of creative expression in this exciting talk.*
