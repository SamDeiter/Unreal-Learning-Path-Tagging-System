# Building Bigger: Changing Your Workflow for Building Worlds instead of Scenes | Unreal Fest 2023

*You can build a scene, but can you build a world? This talk delves into the tools, workflow nuances, and best practices involved in developing a large, open world in Unreal Engine 5. Topics include getting the most out of One File Per Actor, how to handle co-development within large teams, and common pitfalls to avoid. We’ll also walk through a slate of simple tips and tricks to streamline your world building.*
