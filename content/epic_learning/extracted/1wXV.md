# Top 10 Mistakes Made by Unity Teams When Switching to Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores the top mistakes developers make when switching from Unity to Unreal Engine—and how to avoid them.

When teams change engines, they often make assumptions and educated guesses that can get them in trouble.

In this video, Chris Murphy provides a whistlestop tour of the most common mistakes he's helped developers correct during his eight years of aiding teams transition from Unity to Unreal Engine.
