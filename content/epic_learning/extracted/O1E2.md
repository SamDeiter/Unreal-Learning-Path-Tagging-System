# The Infinite Dimensions of the Eiffel Tower’s Digital Twin | Unreal Fest 2023

*Immerse yourself in the groundbreaking world of the Eiffel Tower’s digital twin—a paradigm shift in the realm of digital monuments. In this session, we delve into the birth of this living and breathing virtual counterpart, unlocking a new dimension of monetization, gamification, and socialization possibilities.

Join us as we explore the boundless opportunities presented by the digital twin, enabling monetization strategies that extend far beyond the physical tower's capacity. From exclusive access to the top floor, to exhilarating jetpack adventures, discover how the virtual Eiffel Tower fosters a thriving economy within its immersive world. Learn how gamification elements engage and captivate users, creating an interactive and unforgettable experience. Witness the integration of AI-driven avatars that guide visitors, providing real-time information and personalized interactions and facilitating social connections within this digital ecosystem.*
