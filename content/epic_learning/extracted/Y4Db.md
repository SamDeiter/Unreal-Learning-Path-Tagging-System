# Visualizing Data for Digital Twins in UE5 | Unreal Fest 2024

*In this talk, Cityscape Digital will showcase case studies in which Unreal Engine is used as the core communication tool for complex aggregated and/or live data for AEC development/data science use cases. 

They will present a  demonstration that talks through the design and development challenges of the applications deployed via Pixel Streaming. Attendees will learn how to harness the power of Unreal Engine in a variety of contexts, including:

- as a complex data insights comms tool;
- in bridging the communication gap for broader audiences;
- as a visual-first flexible platform with enough depth and integration capability to tackle problems at scale.*
