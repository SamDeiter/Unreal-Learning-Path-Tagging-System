# Technical Animation Tools | A-COM Animation Sample | Lesson 4

*Experience the process of creating cinematic animation inside Unreal Engine through a complete sample project featuring stylized characters developed by Agora Studio. This course guides you through professional animation workflows—organizing your project, shaping the rhythm of cinematic shots, and using nested level sequences to streamline production. You’ll learn to refine environments, balance world assets with shot-specific elements, and work confidently with cameras and control rigs for precise, expressive results. With hands-on practice using modular character rigs and advanced animation tools, you’ll gain the skills to bring rich performances and polished storytelling to life in real time.*
