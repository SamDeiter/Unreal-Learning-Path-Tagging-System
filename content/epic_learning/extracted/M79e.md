# Electric Dreams: From an Idea to a Demo | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this session, you’ll learn more about the Electric Dreams demo, and find out how a simple idea could be successfully taken to another level using new features in Unreal Engine 5. We’ll spend time taking an in-depth look at the vehicle suspension, tire deformation, and water simulation created with Chaos Physics, as well as touching on the Procedural Content Generation (PCG) framework and Substrate material authoring system.*
