# Content Any Way, Anywhere: Shaping Transmedia Content for Distribution on Any Device, in Any Media | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which Nexus Studios explores how real-time technology is reshaping the landscape of animation and audience engagement. 

As animation creators, Nexus Director Mike Anderson and Executive Producer Diego Rosner aim to connect with audiences in meaningful ways that go beyond traditional avenues. 

Driven by a desire to create new gateways of discovery, they are leveraging real-time technology to revolutionize how content is produced and consumed. In this session, they delve into a series of innovative projects that exemplify the power of real-time character animation and demonstrate how these technologies open the door to entirely new creative opportunities for brands, commissioners, and storytellers, enabling agile, bespoke content that resonates with audiences across various platforms. 

They also discuss the importance of fostering a two-way dialogue between brands and consumers in an era where engagement is rapidly evolving*
