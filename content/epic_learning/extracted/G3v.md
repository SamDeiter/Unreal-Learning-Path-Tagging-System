# RealityCapture Workflow by Quixel

*This course dives into asset alignment using RealityCapture.*


## Aligning Images in RealityCapture

After the scan data is processed, the data is imported into reconstruction software where it is aligned (image by image) into the shape of the original subject. The software then produces a 3D model from the alignment. This course covers aligning the images and reconstructing a 3D model.

The purpose of this document is to introduce the photographer or scanning team to the concepts of photogrammetry. The goal is to provide a base level of knowledge necessary to understand and complete photogrammetry process‑workflows (pipelines).
