# Epic Secondary Educator Summit- Oct 2024

*Join us for our fall Epic Secondary Educator Summit on Oct 12, 2024. Our innovative lineup of educators will share the great work they’re doing integrating interactive 3D technology into their learning spaces. Interactive 3D content development is providing countless opportunities for students who want to explore new careers in film, games, advertising, architecture, engineering, and more. Throughout the summit, you’ll get to see how your peers are integrating this immersive technology in their curricula. In the process, they’ll help to demystify the tools behind this fast-growing technology, so you can learn how you can bring this technology to your students without the need to be an expert yourself.*
