# Workshop | Rigging in Unreal Engine

*Unlock your potential in the Rigging in Unreal Engine Workshop! Our mission, led by the Unreal Engine team, is to empower riggers and technical artists with practical, production-ready skills using Unreal Engine's Control Rig. You will move toward Hands-On Mastery by building a complete animation-ready modular biped rig from the ground up.

Expect powerful Practical Applications through workflows and techniques that are immediately applicable in real production pipelines. You'll also have the chance for Creative Exploration as you experiment with rigging systems, from procedural controls to deformers and physics.*
