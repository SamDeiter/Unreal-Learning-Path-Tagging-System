# Building a Data-Driven Interior Configurator Using Lumen and Nanite | Unreal Fest Orlando 2025

*Cabin Creator is an interior configuration tool used by Gulfstream Aerospace’s sales, design, refurbishment, and customer delivery executive teams. Originally developed to facilitate a continued ability to interact with customers during the COVID 19 crisis, Cabin Creator has expanded over the past five years to become a robust and invaluable tool embedded within the enterprise, generating millions of dollars in revenue through the direct sales of new aircraft, refurbishment upsells, and upgraded material and styling options. 

In this session recorded at Unreal Fest Orlando 2025, Josh Triplett and Brian Toler of Gulfstream Aerospace step through the processes used to create a modular, data-driven configurator, a responsive UI, and the use of Lumen and Nanite to power a fully dynamic interior. The session also includes a demonstration of Cabin Creator.
