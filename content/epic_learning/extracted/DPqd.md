# Project Atlante: An Experiment in Building Earth-Scale Worlds with UE Tools Only | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which we dive into the Unreal Engine simulation demo Project Atlante. 

Project Atlante is an Epic Games initiative that aims to promote the adoption of UE for next-generation simulators across automotive, civil engineering, transportation, aerospace, and defense—and by extension, for all types of digital twins.

In this session, we explore the challenges and solutions around streaming Earth-scale worlds from content streaming to the production of open worlds from real-life geospatial data, passing through chunking and packaging along the way.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
