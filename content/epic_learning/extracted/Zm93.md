# Epic Secondary Educator Summit- Oct 2025

*Join us for our fall Epic Secondary Educator Summit on October 25, 2025. Our fall summit features a dynamic group of educators who are doing amazing work with interactive 3D technology in their classrooms. From animation and game design to engineering and virtual storytelling, 3D content is opening doors for students to engage with real-world career paths in innovative and exciting ways.
During the summit, you'll hear from educators who are successfully using interactive technology in their instruction. They’ll share practical strategies, creative examples, and tips for getting started. Whether you're curious or already experimenting, this summit is your chance to get inspired, build your confidence, and take the next step in bringing interactive 3D learning to your students.*
