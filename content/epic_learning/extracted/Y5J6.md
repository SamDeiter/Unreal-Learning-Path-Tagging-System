# Baked River Simulations - Overview and Quick Start

*This is a quickstart guide to using the river simulation and baking workflow.*

## 


## 

- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'River Simulation- A set of Niagara based fluid solvers capable of simulating water flowing down deformed terrain.\xa0 This includes foam, spray, and advected surface detail waves.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Baking Pipeline - The river simulations are configured and controlled by a new actor, which can also bake rivers to a static texture, which is used for rendering and physics.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Rendering - Both live and baked simulations as well as the secondary passes (advected surface waves, foam, etc) can be rendered either with the Water Plugin or the Niagara System directly.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Chaos Integration- The baked river can apply forces on rigid bodies so they can float down rivers in a realistic manner.\xa0 This is the primary mechanism that allows for novel gameplay methods.'}]


## 

- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Make sure the WaterAdvanced plugin is enabled.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Create a map or open one that has a Landscape in it.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Make a Water Body River and move the spline points in a way that has a downhill flow and is interesting.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Add a Shallow Water River Actor to the map.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Under Source River Water Bodies on the Shallow Water River, add an entry for the Water Body River you created earlier.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Hit the Reset button on the Shallow Water River.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Wait for the entire sim to run, then hit the Bake button.\xa0 To make it run faster, increase Speed to 10 and Num Substeps to 10 before baking.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Change Render State to Water Component with Baked Sim to see your baked simulation.'}]

- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Create a Cube actor and position slightly above the water at the top of the river.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Turn Simulate Physics on.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Set DefaultBuoyancyPhysicalMaterial as the Phys Material Override.\xa0 You may have to enable showing Engine and Plugin content to see this.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Make sure the Cube is set to Block WaterBodies in the Collision Response settings.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Set the WaterBody to Query and Probe for Collision Enabled, and make sure that it is set to Block whatever type your Cube is (ie: Physics Body).'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Either simulate or PIE and you should see your Cube float down the river.'}]

- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Make sure “Use Default Shallow Water Subsystem” is enabled in project settings'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'Validate that the player pawns and/or vehicles have physics assets associated with them.\xa0 These are used for generating ripples.'}]
- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'When a player or vehicle enters the water during the game, you should see ripples'}]
