# Traditional VFX Assets Versus Real-Time Assets | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this session, we’ll highlight the key differences between building assets for real-time rendering as compared to traditional visual effects. Discover techniques for asset optimization, procedural content generation, LODs, Nanite, texturing, and look development, as well as overall asset design considerations.
