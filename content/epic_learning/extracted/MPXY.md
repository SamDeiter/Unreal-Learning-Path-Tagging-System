# Bringing 'Fera: The Sundered Tribes' to Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which Massive Damage Inc. discuss the challenges and solutions their 20-strong team encountered while bringing their first Unreal Engine game to market in a competitive, post-COVID industry. 

‘Fera: The Sundered Tribes’ is an open-world, multiplayer survival RPG that combines village simulation with high-speed aerial combat against gigantic monsters. 

Check out the video for an insight into the ""why and how"" of Fera's vibrant art direction, as well as how Massive Damage approaches the intersection of game design and development, covering topics such as: 

Adapting Unreal's lighting model and utilizing Lumen to achieve a distinctive visual style 
How Unreal Engine enabled an art team to stand out in a crowded market  
Maximizing iteration speeds while maintaining and extending designer-driven tools

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
