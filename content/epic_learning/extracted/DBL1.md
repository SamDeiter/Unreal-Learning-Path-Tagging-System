# Unreal Fest 2025: Heads & Tails with Sequencer

*Master the Unreal Sequencer and revolutionize your creative process! This class shows you how to maximize flexibility and organization, giving you more creative power without extra effort. Learn how to handle real-world challenges, like adapting shot lengths for multilingual voiceovers, while keeping your workflow smooth and efficient. 

You’ll dive into essential skills like adding heads and tails to shots, applying burn-ins, layering levels, using the Take Recorder, and linking lights—all to ensure your visuals and timing are perfectly synchronized. By the end, you’ll have the tools to make your projects more dynamic and adaptable than ever.*
