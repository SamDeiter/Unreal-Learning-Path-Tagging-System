# Unlocking Creativity | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this panel discussion, experts from top creative studios will join Epic Games to explore the new and exciting opportunities for leveraging IP across transmedia experiences. 

Today, your stories are no longer confined to their original medium. Characters that start out as traditional linear animation can be transported into playable, interactive experiences for your audience to enjoy. You can even open up your IP and enable fans to create their own content rooted in the world you've created.

Join us to find out about the evolution that's going on in storytelling—and get inspiration on how you can engage and delight your own audience with transmedia IP.*
