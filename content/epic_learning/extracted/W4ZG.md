# Revolutionizing Brand Digital Heritage: Harnessing Unreal Engine for Innovation | Unreal Fest 2024

*In this panel, professionals from various sectors (advertising, entertainment, automotive and manufacturing) will share their feedback and insights into what Unreal Engine enables them to achieve in terms of creating and leveraging their digital assets. 

Unreal Engine users will gain a concrete understanding of how they can optimally utilize assets created across various media and platforms. Marketing, communication, innovation, design, and R&amp;D experts from brands will discover how they can adapt their company assets to maximize ROI.*
