## 📁 Demonstrating Animation Blueprints and AI for Gameplay Designers Overview

Explore the close relationship between Animation Blueprints and artificial intelligence, two important game design topics, and build a basic enemy character AI through animation assets, montages, Blueprints, and behavior trees.


### RUNNING TIME

1 hour 55 minutes


### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN


### You'll learn how to:

- Control the animation of a character with animation assets.
- Use an Animation Blueprint to effect the players gameplay.
- Trigger logic and functionality with Animation Montages.
- Create an AI enemy that can spot the player with Blackboard and Behavior Trees.
