# Linear Content for YouTube: Learnings from The Amazing Digital Circus and Murder Drones | Unreal Fest Gold Coast 2024

*Glitch Productions is an indie animation company that distributes direct to audiences on YouTube with over eight million subscribers, five shows, and 720 millions collective views. In this session, Glitch will share learnings and pipeline advice from its shows The Amazing Digital Circus and Murder Drones.

Come along for an insight into creating stylised cartoon expressions for characters; lighting and rendering tips and tricks; advice on budgeting and planning; and much more.*
