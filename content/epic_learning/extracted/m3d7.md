# Enter the Cockpit: The World’s First Visual System to Train Airline Pilots with Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which CAE explains how complex simulators are built to recreate the most realistic flight experience, enabling pilot training and performance assessment in the most difficult flight conditions.

CAE is a world leader in high-fidelity aircraft simulators to train commercial and military pilots around the world. It has integrated UE5 into its simulator: learn about the specific challenges associated with this and how Unreal Engine contributes to CAE’s mission to make our skies safer.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
