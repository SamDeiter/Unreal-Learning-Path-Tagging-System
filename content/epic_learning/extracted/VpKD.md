# Introduction to VAD Optimization for Real-Time Production

*Optimization is the process of making something (such as a design, system or real-time media) as fully functional as possible. Regarding using Unreal for real-time production, the primary concern is tailoring the geometry, lights and materials of a project to target a specific frame rate threshold. Adjustments can be to the project settings to affect the global qualities of the project or by editing the assets themselves to enhance performance. Preceding technical requirements for assets, decisions about final quality and format need to be made by the Director and the Art Department.*
