# Designing Automotive HMI using XR | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this session, we’ll explore the exciting applications of Extended Reality (XR) technologies in the design process of automotive Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI) using Unreal Engine. XR encompasses a range of immersive technologies, including Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Mixed Reality (MR), which can be harnessed within the powerful Unreal Engine ecosystem to revolutionize the automotive HMI design workflow. 

Whether you are an experienced HMI designer or new to XR, this session will provide you with valuable insights and practical tips for incorporating these technologies into your automotive HMI design workflow. By the end, you will have a solid understanding of how Unreal Engine and XR can enhance and simplify the design process, enabling you to create exceptional and immersive HMIs.*
