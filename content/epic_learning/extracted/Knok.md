# Architectural Epics: Exploring the Hillside Sample Project with Neoscape and Google Pixel Streaming | Unreal Fest 2023

*"Join us for a panel discussion on the iconic architectural sample project Hillside, which aimed to fulfill the 60-year-old vision of Habitat67 in collaboration with Safdie Architects. In this session, you’ll have the opportunity to hear from Epic Games as they delve into the fascinating journey of architectural visualization using Unreal Engine and provide a glimpse of what lies ahead. 

Additionally, we’ll chat with Neoscape and the Google Cloud team as they share insights on the project’s construction and deployment to tens of thousands of people around the world."*
