# Creating a Common Simulation Core Platform to Build Complex Training Scenarios with Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2023

*Lockheed Martin is leveraging the power of UE5 to transform their simulation and training technology pipeline. Find out how their unified simulation engine baseline is being managed and used to create multiple innovative defense focused technologies, including an integration of human-worn sensor fusion used to properly train ground soldiers, and development of a new high-fidelity visual engine to train the next generation of helicopter pilots.*
