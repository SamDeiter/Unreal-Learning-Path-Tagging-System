# Mind The Gap: Transitioning From Unity to Unreal

*This Learning Lab is geared towards Unity3D developers who are interested in transitioning to Unreal Engine, but feel intimidated by learning a new program, migrating over assets and content to a new engine, or for those who keep getting tripped up by competing design philosophies, nomenclature, and pipelines. In this session we will gain new understanding of how these programs align and differ, learn to utilize a number of core features to Unreal Engine, and explore how to start leveraging the unique strengths those features afford.*
