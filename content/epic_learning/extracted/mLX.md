# Fundamentals of Materials

*In this course from Epic Online Learning, we walk through the fundamentals of Materials. Learn how to use Material nodes to craft a Material, apply unique Materials to Actors through Material Instances, and make Material creation easier with Material Functions. Come discover how to make creating exceptional Materials simpler than ever!*


## 📁 Fundamentals of Materials Overview


### RUNNING TIME

42 minutes


### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

You'll learn how to:

- Identify what a Material is and the different parts that are used to create it.
- Utilize Material nodes to create a Material that can be applied to an object.
- Apply unique Materials to other actor instances by using Material Instances.
- Make Material creation easier with Material Functions.
- Explore a Material's primary properties by examining each property's impact.
