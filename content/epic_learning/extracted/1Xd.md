## RealityCapture: Making 3D Models with your smartphone!

Learn how to 3D scan an object with your smartphone using Reality Capture and incorporate it as a 3D model in Unreal Engine.

This tutorial will cover;

00:00 - An overview of the tutorial 
00:25 - Installation of Reality Capture
01:16 - Tips and techniques for capturing images for a 3D scan using your smartphone.
03:02 - Creating the 3D model in Reality Capture
08:06 - Cleanup topology in Maya
10:50 - Texture Reprojection in Reality Capture
14:59 - Importing and using the 3D model in Unreal Engine

In just 20 minutes, you'll learn the workflow: capturing images with your smartphone, creating a 3D model using Reality Capture with the captured images, and importing the 3D model into Unreal Engine.

Hope you enjoy the tutorial.
