# Lighting and AI with Paper 2D

*In this course from the Epic Online Learning team, apply dynamic lighting to Paper 2D levels, create an enemy AI that can move intelligently, and use a damage system for your enemy AI and player to interact.*


## 📁 Lighting and AI with Paper 2D Overview

Looking to upgrade your Paper 2D projects? Learn how to dynamically light Paper 2D environments and give players some control of the camera. We will also create an AI that intelligently moves around the level and can damage players, and a player attack to damage the enemy AI.


### RUNNING TIME

1 hour 33 minutes


### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN You'll learn how to:

- Enable dynamic lighting on Paper 2D assets by modifying rendering and material options.
- Use Blueprints to create an AI character which can move without falling along a platform and do damage to a player.
- Implement Unreal Engine's built-in damage system to send and receive damage from players and AI characters.
- Apply damage to an AI character with Unreal Engine's damage system when the player lands on the enemy from above.
- Create a Blueprint system which affects camera position based on input from the player.
