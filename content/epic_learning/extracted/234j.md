# Choosing How Your Game Animation Feels | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores how Unreal Engine 5.5 levels up gameplay animation with Choosers.

This talk deep dives on Choosers in and outside of animation, root motion-driven Motion Matching, and many of our Motion Matching improvements coming in UE 5.5. 

We also explore updates to the Game Animation Sample including new features and over 100 new animations and talk about the future of gameplay animation beyond UE 5.5.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
