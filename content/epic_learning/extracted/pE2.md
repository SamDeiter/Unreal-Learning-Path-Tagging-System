## 📁 Introducing Post Processing Overview

This course steps through implementing cinematic cameras, controlling your final image, color grading your scene, using LUTs, and outputting your final image to multiple industry-supported formats.


### RUNNING TIME

1 hour 9 minutes


### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN


### You'll learn how to:

- Change the visual style of a scene with a Post Process Volume.
- Use realistic exposure control and high-quality Post Processing effects by enabling their Project Settings.
- Implement cinematic cameras into a project.
- Add artistic effects with layered Post Process Volumes.
- Color Grade and tonemap a scene using a Post Process Volume and LUTs.
