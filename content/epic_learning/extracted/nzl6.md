# Real-Time 3D HMI for the High-End Yacht Industry | Unreal Fest 2024

*In this session, Applied will showcase how they developed and delivered HMI software for the luxury yacht industry.

Explore how the team used Unreal Engine to develop a 3D application installed on a touchscreen in the cockpit of the yacht that enables the crew to see a 3D visualization of the boat and control the status of more than 600 variables of the vessel’s various systems.
  
This session will demonstrate to manufacturers and developers how it’s possible to use UE to build complex and mission-critical HMIs—and that this is not only achievable by big and established companies but also by smaller teams without compromising on quality, complexity, and functionality.*
