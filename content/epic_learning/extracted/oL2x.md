# The State of Unreal Automotive: From Visualization to HMI Development | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores the key beats and achievements so far in 2024 for Unreal Engine and the Epic ecosystem in the automotive industry. 

This talk focuses on automotive visualization and HMI development, with the Epic automotive leadership team exploring the current state of auto-focused technology in UE 5.5 and what this means for the industry. There’s also a sneak peek into the roadmap for 2025 and beyond.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
