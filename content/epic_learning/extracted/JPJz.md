# From VFX to VAD: Artists’ Journeys with Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores the journeys of Pixomondo artists who transitioned from traditional VFX to mastering Unreal Engine as Virtual Art Department (VAD) generalists. 

In this session, Pixomondo artists share their experiences, challenges, and successes in adapting to new tools and techniques. They highlight innovative projects they've worked on and talk about their contributions to virtual production and the future of filmmaking. 

Watch to gain insights into the artists’ learning process, their creative evolution, and the impactful content they’ve produced using UE.
