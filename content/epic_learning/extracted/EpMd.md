# Dune: Awakening by Funcom | State of Unreal | GDC 2024

*Funcom delved into the mechanics of their open-world survival game Dune: Awakening, inspired by Denis Villeneuve's record-breaking and critically acclaimed Dune films, which are based on Frank Herbert's novels. Funcom also shared how two time Academy Award-winning cinematographer Greig Fraser used Unreal Engine for planning and pre-production on Dune: Part Two, illustrating how storytelling processes behind making films and games continue to merge.
