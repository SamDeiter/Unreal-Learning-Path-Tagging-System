# Simulating Real-Time Photorealistic Denim Treatments for the Fashion Industry | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session by WOPTAG from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 and learn how Unreal Engine not only solves the problem of digitizing denim, but also provides a highly versatile, creative, and intuitive application that can be used in every step of the denim design process.  

Denim is a timeless fashion item found in every collection from luxury brands to high-street labels. Fashion's need to be more sustainable and environmentally friendly is well known and digitalization is a key solution. 

The digitalization of 'denim' design and production processes however, despite its substantial environmental footprint due to vast amounts of water and chemicals used in production, has largely been overlooked due to its complexity and variety—until now! 

This development has the potential to lead to a drastic decrease in manufacturing of physical samples and would open the door to a vastly more sustainable and environmentally friendly approach to designing and producing denim products.
