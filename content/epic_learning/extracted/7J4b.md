# The Importance of the Art Department in Visual Storytelling | Unreal Fest 2023

*This session will discuss the art department’s role in film and television production and how Unreal Engine and Twinmotion fit in. 

The art department is fundamental to the process of filmmaking. It’s where production decisions are made and accurately visualizing those decisions is critical to communicating and coordinating with the other film departments. 

Join our panel of virtual production and art department experts as we explore the history and evolution of the art department—and the role real-time technology plays in the future of filmmaking.*
