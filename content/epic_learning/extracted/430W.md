# Making AAA-Quality Cinematics with a Small Team | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which emptyvessel inc. covers how they leveraged MetaHuman and Unreal Engine to create a pipeline for authoring high-quality characters and content quickly. 

You’ll get an insight into how they achieved fantastic results with a small team without losing flexibility and while retaining desired art direction. 

They also dive into Nanite and how they used it for characters and first-person weapons; things to look out for; and how they optimized and sped up their workflow.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
