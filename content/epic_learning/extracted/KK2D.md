# Photoreal Digital Actors from Capture to Final Render: a ‘Blue Dot’ Case Study | Unreal Fest 2023

*As Unreal Engine continues to raise the bar for real-time photoreal worlds, there is more and more demand to populate these worlds with photoreal digital humans. Recently, Epic’s 3Lateral team put together an impressive showcase of these digital human technologies in a short film called Blue Dot. Join us as we break down the technologies and artistry responsible for creating digital Radivoje Bukvić for Blue Dot while trying to stay true to the actor's performance at every stage.*
