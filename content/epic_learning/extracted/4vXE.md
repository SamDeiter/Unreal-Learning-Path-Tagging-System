# Unlocking the Potential of Immersive Air Traffic Control Training | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this presentation, Sonia Mina will speak about Adacel’s ongoing mission to improve Air Traffic Control (ATC) simulation realism in a training environment, and why the company chose to transition to a new image generation (IG) solution, AeroScene, powered by Unreal Engine.

She’ll give a brief overview about the importance of immersive experience in air traffic control simulation and training, and discuss how it increases learning retention and eases the transition to the real-world ATC environment. She’ll also reveal how customers’ expectations fueled the IG change at Adacel.

You’ll gain an understanding of how AeroScene leverages the power of Unreal Engine to deliver greater realism in ATC training, as well as learning about the solution’s expanded capabilities to meet the ever-growing customer demands for real-life-like immersive training experiences.*
