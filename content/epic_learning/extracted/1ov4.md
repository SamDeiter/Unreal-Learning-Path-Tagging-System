# The Best Template You're Not Using: Maximizing the Collab Viewer | Unreal Fest Orlando 2025

*Want the fastest way to build a multiplayer experience that works across desktop and VR platforms? Look no further than the Collaborative Viewer Template—but then look a little further and let's customize the heck out of it.

In this session recorded at Unreal Fest Orlando 2025, Alex Coulombe of Agile Lens showcases the many modular components, design review capabilities, and various navigation options this underused template offers; with a little elbow grease, you can easily add a wide range of features, from additional menu options to worldwide multiplayer, Pixel Streaming, and even cross-platform compatibility. 

And while this template is developed with enterprise in mind (it has architectural and automotive variants), this session also covers how to use the basic structure of the project for a wide variety of industries, including games!

Learn more about Unreal Engine here: unrealengine.com*
