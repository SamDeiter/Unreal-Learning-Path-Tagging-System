# Physical Visualization of Global Datasets with CBRE and Imerza | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores how Imerza developed a global digital twin platform for the real-estate giant CBRE and created a marketing center that physically changes shape with the virtual environment. 

Learn how Imerza uses Unreal to show massive data sets with Niagara, populate the globe with dynamic geometry, setup advanced projections, and communicate with robotics in this world-first initiative.
