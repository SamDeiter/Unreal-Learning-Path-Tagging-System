# Epic Secondary Educator Summit

*On August 15, 2023 we hosted our first ever Epic Secondary Educator Summit. An amazing and diverse group of educators shared the great work they’re doing integrating interactive 3D technology into their learning spaces.

Interactive 3D content development is providing countless opportunities for students who want to explore new careers in film, games, advertising, architecture, engineering, and more. Throughout the summit, you’ll hear how your peers are using this immersive technology in their curricula.

In the process, they’ll help to demystify the tools behind this fast-growing technology, so you can learn how you can bring this technology to your students without the need to be an expert yourself.

We hope you find the resources and videos provided here to be helpful on your teaching and learning journey.*
