# Niagara Examples Pack

*The Niagara Examples Pack can be found on FAB. It contains FX specific assets which can be used as is, or used as a series of examples to base your own assets on.*


## Niagara Examples Pack: Introduction

An introduction to the Niagara Examples Pack.

The Niagara Examples Content Pack is a collection of Niagara systems, blueprints, materials and textures that you can import directly into your projects. Use them as is or adapt them to your needs. Open them up and use them as reference for your own learning and experimentation. We have built them with best practices in mind including Effect Types, Data Channels and Scalability throughout.

The pack includes a Gallery Level where we demonstrate some of the ways VFX assets can be incorporated into your projects. Example weapon systems and foot step animation notifies are included. Pick-ups, dissolve effects, explosions, teleporters and projectiles are ready for you to explore. Here is a list of some of the examples available:
