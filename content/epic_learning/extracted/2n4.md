# How to Light an Exterior Architectural Scene

*In this course from Epic Online Learning, discover methods to light your exterior scenes with real-life lighting as reference. Control the final look of your scene with Post Process Volumes, emulate real-life exterior lighting with Lumen and Atmospheric Components, and adjust your final scene with volumetric and post-processing effects.*


## 📁How to Light an Exterior Architectural Scene Overview

Use some of the built-in features of Unreal Engine to realistically light any exterior scene! Let’s discuss ways to create a system of lights and volumes that can adjust every aspect of the final look, with real-life lighting conditions as our reference.


### RUNNING TIME

40 minutes


### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN


### You'll learn how to:

- Set up a scene with the Lighting Actors necessary for realistic exterior lighting.
- Control the final visual look and quality of the scene with a Post Process Volume.
- Emulate realistic exterior lighting conditions using Lumen and Atmospheric Components.
- Identify potential issues that detract from the overall visual quality of the final image.
- Create a more realistic-looking scene with volumetric and post-processing effects.
