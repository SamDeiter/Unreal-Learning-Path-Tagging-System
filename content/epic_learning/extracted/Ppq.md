*Author Joel Bradley teaches you essential Blueprint concepts as you create four practical examples you can use in your AEC projects, including automatic doors, connected switched lights, material switching, construction scripts, and more.*


## Welcome to Blueprints by Example

Author Joel Bradley introduces you to this course and the four practical Blueprints projects designed for AEC visualization projects. He gives you an overview of each of the projects, as well as the critical concepts you will learn while building them.
