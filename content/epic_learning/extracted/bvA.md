# Image Calibration by Quixel

*The Image Processing course details the process of image correction primarily using Abobe Lightroom.*


## Processing RAW Images

After scanning a subject, the collection of scan images is called "scan data". The scan data undergoes a quality review to remove blurry or out-of-focus images and then the remaining images are processed. This course covers the image corrections necessary to produce high quality reconstructions.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to introduce the photographer or scanning team to the concepts of photogrammetry. The goal is to provide a base level of knowledge necessary to understand and complete photogrammetry process‑workflows (pipelines).


## Scope

The scope of this document includes an overview of the photogrammetry method (scanning). It is recommended to read Introduction to Photogrammetry prior to attempting these techniques.


## Audience

This document is intended for anyone interested in or already practicing photogrammetry that understands the advanced features and settings of digital cameras.
