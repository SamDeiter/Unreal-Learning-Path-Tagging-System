# Blurring the Lines between Animation and Gaming with Unreal Engine and Fortnite | Unreal Fest 2024

*In this talk, film and game director HaZ Dulull will give us a deep dive into how he and his team at production company HaZimation took a strategic approach to transmedia when producing original content for film and games, and how one IP turned into a Fortnite game—Moontopia—using UEFN. 

This session will break down how HaZimation were able to pivot from animated series development into a game IP thanks to UEFN / Fortnite.*
