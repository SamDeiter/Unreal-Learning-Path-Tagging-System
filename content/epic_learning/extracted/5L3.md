# Implementing UI for Level Design

*In this course from Epic Online learning, discover how to use objective markers to guide players through complex levels and sequential events. Create UMG objective markers to display on a player’s Heads-Up Display (HUD) and call functions in a Widget Blueprint to trigger events in the level.*


## 📁 Implementing UI for Level Design Overview

Objective markers are a great way to orient players on a complex map, alert them of the next step of a narrative sequence, or entice players to explore towards a distant destination. In this course, you’ll learn how to create waypoint icons that only reveal themselves as the player progresses through a level. We will also use relevant text to give players context for our narrative or help guide them to navigate a map and solve problems in sequence.
