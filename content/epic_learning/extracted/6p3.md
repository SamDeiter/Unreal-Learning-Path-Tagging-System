# Habitat 67: Unpacked

*In this course from Epic Online Learning, we dive into the Hillside Project and explore the LiDAR and Photogrammetry assets used to bring this virtual world to life. Learn how to import, clean, and render the LiDAR content in Unreal Engine, as well as the Photogrammetry content.*


## 📁 Habitat 67: Unpacked Overview

Unlock the power of precision 3D-mapping with the Hillside Project! Discover how to integrate LiDAR and Photogrammetry assets into your Unreal Engine projects, bringing them to life with easy clean up, animation, and rendering tools.


### RUNNING TIME

22 minutes


### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN


### You'll learn how to:

- Gain an introduction to the LiDAR and Photogrammetry assets used in the Hillside Project.
- Import, clean, animate, and render the LiDAR sample content in Unreal Engine.
- Import, clean, and modularize the Photogrammetry sample content in Unreal Engine.
