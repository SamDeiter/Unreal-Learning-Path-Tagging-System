# Building Immersive Real-World Digital Twins with Unreal, Twinmotion and Cesium | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores the use of Unreal Engine, Twinmotion, Cesium for Unreal, and 3D Tiles in digital twin projects. 

Architecture, engineering, and construction projects increasingly need to communicate and get buy-in from stakeholders on how a proposed design fits into and impacts the real-world environment. This can lead to better decisions for our communities.  

Using Unreal Engine, Twinmotion, Cesium for Unreal, and 3D Tiles, Cesium demonstrates examples of real-world projects built from multiple design sources and integrated into a full-scale accurate high-fidelity real-world 3D geospatial context.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
