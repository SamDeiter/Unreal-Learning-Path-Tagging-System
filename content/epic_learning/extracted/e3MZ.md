# Interoperability: The Epitome of Better Together! | Unreal Fest 2023

*Gone are the days of locked-down, proprietary solutions that purport to solve complex real-world problems. The solutions of today demand all sectors and segments of industry, government, and academia to collaborate in creating flexible, interoperable, and scalable solutions. We are now beginning to see the positive results of investments in collaboration that are accelerating the development of significantly modernized solutions ranging from hyperrealistic simulations for aerospace and defense, to the creation of dynamic and distributed digital twins enabling unprecedented insights into the world around us.*
