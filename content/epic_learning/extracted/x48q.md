# Unleashing UE Motion Design: Packaging and Beyond | Unreal Fest Orlando 2025

*In this session recorded at Unreal Fest Orlando 2025, Denys Dubinin of Geotech and Danny Tello of Emissive Labs explore motion design in Unreal Engine.

Geotech, as an official Epic Games development partner, helped architect and develop Unreal Engine’s Motion Design tool from the start, and remain active contributors to its evolution.

In 2024, in collaboration with Emissive Labs, Geotech took the Motion Design Toolset to the next level by developing a live-events application, venue4D, designed to run Unreal Engine motion graphics in stadiums and arenas.

Denys and Danny demonstrate how to package your motion design project and generate a game build; control scenes in a packaged game using network APIs; optimize performance for demanding scenarios; and implement your own extensions and package them for distribution. 

Denys also shows how to apply AAA game development best practices to motion design and real-time graphics for media & entertainment.
