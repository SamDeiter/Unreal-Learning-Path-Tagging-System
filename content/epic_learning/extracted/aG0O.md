# Using Animation in a Production Environment in UE5 and UEFN | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this session, we’ll take a deep dive into animation workflows in Unreal Engine. We’ll review the most recent 5.3 engine release and showcase some of the improvements we’ve made over the past couple releases. We will then take a look at some behind-the-scenes footage from a couple of our 2023 GDC demos, and go over some workflows in both UE and UEFN. We’ll conclude with a peek at what our future roadmap may look like, followed by a brief Q&amp;A.*
