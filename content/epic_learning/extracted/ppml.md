# Real-Time Audiovisual Mechanics: An Epic Chat with deadmau5 | Unreal Fest Orlando 2025

*Come for an engaging chat with Joel Zimmerman—the legendary producer, musician, and DJ known as deadmau5—about the intersection of real-time audio, music, and graphics rendering with Unreal Engine 5. Zimmerman recently released Meowingtons Simulator for PC, a ragdoll dancing simulator, under his Oberha5li Studios banner. Join Epic Games Director of Unreal Engine Audio Aaron McLeran and Senior Audio Programmer Max Hayes, along with moderator Miles Perkins, Director of Client and Industry Relations for Media and Entertainment.*
