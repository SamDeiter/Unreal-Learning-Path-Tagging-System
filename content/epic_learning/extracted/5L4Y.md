# Building the Future of Real Estate Search in 3D | Unreal Fest 2023

*This session will showcase PropVR’s pioneering efforts in revolutionizing the real estate industry through the development of a cutting-edge metaverse experiences for real estate. The session will discuss the PropTech revolution and the integration of real-time experiences into different modules of real estate transactions. 

We’ll cover use of AI in real estate with MetaHumans as we delve into Square Yards’ groundbreaking project for real estate sales AI, the first-ever sales avatar for real estate metaverse. We’ll discuss the significance of real-time experiences in real estate and how PropVR is integrating them into different transaction modules, as well as exploring practical examples and their impact on user engagement. And we’ll explore how PropVR is transforming real estate search and discovery through the development of a groundbreaking metaverse using Unreal Engine.*
