# ICVR’s Real-Time Pipeline | Unreal Fest 2023

*Join ICVR’s Chris Swiatek and Ihar Heneralau for an inside look at the latest version of their real-time production toolkit for animation, ICVFX, and asset production for games. They’ll break down how production tools and automations can be used to trace and manage all data throughout production, integrate with existing tools, and increase efficiency for your current productions and those in the future.*
