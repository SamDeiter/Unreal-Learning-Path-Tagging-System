# Advancing Automotive Visuals with ICVFX in Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2023

*Step into the evolving world of in-camera visual effects (ICVFX), as we spotlight the latest in automotive LED stage content. This panel will delve into the mechanics of integrating vehicles within virtual production stages, emphasizing the specialized content requisites of such pioneering endeavors. 

NantStudios will present their production workflows, offering insights into the creation and optimization of content for diverse segments of automotive commercial production. As we conclude, we’ll cast a spotlight on the future, discussing the next generation of modular LED stages and the integration of automotive motion bases. Join us for a comprehensive exploration of the next chapter in automotive.*
