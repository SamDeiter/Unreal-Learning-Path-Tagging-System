# Creating Shorts and Cinematics as a Solo Creator | Unreal Fest Orlando 2025

*Watch this session recorded at Unreal Fest Orlando 2025 to explore how a single artist can create at a professional level without the resources of a studio or the power of a team behind them.

In this video, Sir Neistadt of Sir Wade Animation shows you how to build yourself an indie pipeline using UE5, how to build up a project without a team, and how to leverage both the Epic ecosystem and its new features, especially Sequencer and other animation tools. He also imparts tips from his experience in media and entertainment to help artists shortcut their onboarding into Unreal Engine and UEFN.

Learn more about using Unreal Engine for film and TV here: unrealengine.com/uses/film-television*
