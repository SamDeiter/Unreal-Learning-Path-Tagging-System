# Advanced Animation Techniques | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 for advanced and professional animators looking to make the transition from Maya or another DCC to animating in Unreal Engine. 

The session begins with the similarities and how to get your bearings, then dives into the weeds of how things work differently in Unreal Engine. 

There are a lot of things you can do in UE that are similar to Maya, but there's often a better way to do it that's unique to Unreal animation workflows—such as the workflow used in Maya called 'Space Switching' which is simplified and easy out of the box in UE. 

We demonstrate some of the fundamental principles of this Space Switching methodology in Unreal Engine and explore how you can use it to extrapolate motion and get easy and free complex motion.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
