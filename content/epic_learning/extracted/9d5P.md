# Turning Millions of Viewers into Players: Converting Our Viral Series into a Video Game | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which the SOKRISPYMEDIA team discuss the ongoing process of turning their 300 million-viewed ‘Chalk Warfare’ YouTube series into a playable game using Unreal Engine 5. 

They go over the full process of migrating mediums, from ideation and audience expectations to the development and pipeline between both formats. 

Watch for a creative and technical talk—and check out some initial looks from the in-development project.
