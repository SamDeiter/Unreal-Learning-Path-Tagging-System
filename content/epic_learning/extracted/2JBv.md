# Blueprints: What, When, Why, and How? | Unreal Fest 2023

*Blueprints can do a lot of things, but when should you use them, and what can you do with them? This session is a full Blueprint A to Z, useful for first-timers right through to experts; we might even have some useful tips and tricks that you didn’t know about! With Unreal Engine 5, we have introduced a host of new Blueprint capabilities, so join us to find out some pieces you may have missed; we’ll also get you up to speed on some exciting new Blueprint features in UE 5.3 and demonstrate how you can use them both within and to create your pipeline.*
