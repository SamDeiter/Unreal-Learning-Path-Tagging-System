# Asset Dependency Chains: The Hidden Danger | Unreal Fest 2022

*When you are dealing with assets, it’s easy for dependency chains to get out of hand. And sadly, you don't always notice them until they become a big problem. In this talk aimed at programmers &amp; blueprinters, you’ll learn about how to spot, analyze, and fix them using tricks we've developed at Lucid Games so you don't make the same mistakes!*
