# Architectural Visualization for Design Strategies on a 120 Year-Old Building | Unreal Fest 2024

*"Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which global design, architecture, engineering, and planning firm HOK reveal how senior architectural conservators for the Centre Block Rehabilitation Project at Canada’s Parliamentary precinct tasked their design team with presenting the final state of several rooms within Centre Block that have been altered over the years, deviating from architect John A. Pearson's original design. 

They dive into how they used Unreal Engine to develop a technical visualization and data workflow, displaying edited photogrammetric meshes with real-time lighting to create a photorealistic 3D environment for client approval.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US"*
