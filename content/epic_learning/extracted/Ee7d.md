# ICVFX / Virtual Production for Short-Form Media | Unreal Fest 2023

*Synapse Virtual Production’s Rich Lee, Louise Baker Lee, and Emily Haldeman will explore the pros and cons of kitbashed and premade assets versus entirely custom-made environments. The team will illustrate how leveraging existing elements culled from the Marketplace, Megascans, and so on, can help avoid unnecessary staffing in pipelines used with traditional VFX, and allow for increased flexibility on a virtual production set when you don’t have that much pre-production time. 

This presentation is designed for anyone interested in virtual production, from VAD creators, Unreal Engine operators, VP supervisors to producers, cinematographers, and directors. It will clearly illustrate that while virtual production has proven its advantages for long-form or episodic media, it also has a variety of underutilized advantages for short-form media.*
