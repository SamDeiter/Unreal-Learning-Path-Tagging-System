# Unlocking the Power of Unreal Engine in the Cloud | Unreal Fest 2023

*Join us to explore running Unreal Engine workloads in the cloud. We'll cover everything from essential concepts and cost considerations, to the latest advancements and real-world examples. Get ready to unleash your Unreal Engine experiences in the cloud, reaching any device while keeping your IP secure. 

In this session, you'll gain a comprehensive understanding of which Unreal Engine workloads align with cloud environments; learn to navigate the choices between containers and VMs; and discover insights into scaling options, the challenges of handling large-scale projects, and clever techniques to optimize costs. 

You'll also learn the exciting challenges and recent advances in running real-world, large-scale Unreal Engine workloads in the cloud that enable us to unveil streaming the Unreal Editor in the cloud. Say hello to remote collaboration, streamlined training workflows, and the freedom to harness the Editor's power on any device—no installations or hardware worries required!*
