# The Art of Virtual Camera in Animation | Unreal Fest 2022

*What does an animated TV show look like when it’s shot like a live action movie? Join the team at Reel FX as they break down the creative and practical uses of virtual cinematography on their original Netflix series rendered entirely in Unreal, Super Giant Robot Brothers! From simulating a live-action camera, to vcam as a real-time communication tool, the team will break down their learnings from the show.*
