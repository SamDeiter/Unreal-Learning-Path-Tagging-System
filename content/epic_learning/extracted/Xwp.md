# Lighting Essential Concepts and Effects

*This course explains the basics of setting up lighting in Unreal Engine. Learn about the different types of lights, how to use them together, and how to add special effects like fog and reflections.*


## Lighting Essential Concepts and Effects: Introduction

In this course we will cover the basics of lighting in Unreal Engine including static and dynamic lighting, indoor and outdoor lighting, and how to combine them. We will also look at how lightmaps are made, and the most important properties.
