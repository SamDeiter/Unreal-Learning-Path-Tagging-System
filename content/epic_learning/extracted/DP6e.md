# Creating Compelling Procedural Music: Composing with UE Audio in “Cycles" | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores the advantages, opportunities, and challenges of creating truly procedural music and sound, with a special focus on the Unreal Engine audio features that make this possible.

Using the audiovisual piece “Cycles” as a case study, we dive deep into the composition process and look at behind-the-scenes examples of how the entire procedural system was built.

We also discuss why now is an incredibly exciting time to be at the intersection of game dev and procedural music composition.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
