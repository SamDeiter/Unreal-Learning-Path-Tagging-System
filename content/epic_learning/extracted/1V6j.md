# GRACE Studio Suite: Building a Comprehensive Web-Based Virtual Production Toolset | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this session, we discuss the unique requirements that broadcast television poses for virtual production, and how automated and templatized tools in a highly engineered Unreal Engine control layer can reduce the footprint from many UE specialists to a single video operator.

You’ll get a complete overview of the GRACE Studio Suite that Disney Entertainment &amp; ESPN Technology’s Creative Automation &amp; Studio Technology (CAST) team developed with ESPN Studio Operations to drive virtual production on the LED Catalyst Stage in the traditional broadcast pipeline.*
