# Exploring the Animation Sample Production Files | A-COM Animation Sample | Lesson 1

*Dive into professional cinematic production using Unreal Engine! This course uses a full sample project featuring stylized characters developed by Agora Studio to guide you through industry animation workflows.

You will learn to manage the project structure, including editing the pace of your cinematic shots and utilizing nested level sequences (sub-sequences) for organization. Explore environmental control by adjusting the master level and understanding how world assets (possessibles) interact with shot-specific elements (spawnables).

Gain crucial skills by adjusting camera setups, using dedicated control rigs, and optimizing your viewports with dual panels for efficient animation. Finally, get hands-on experience with the modular character rigs, practicing setup and exploring controls for IK/FK switching and fully rigged faces. Prepare to dig into the animation tools and bring these high-quality assets to life.*
