# Large-Scale 3D Digital Twins with AI and Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2023

*Creating 3D Digital Twins is a challenging and time-consuming task, requiring the combination of many technologies and in particular an extensive use of the latest AI technologies. Join us to find out how AI and Unreal Engine are being used to enhance large-scale 3D digital twins that are being built by Blackshark.AI leveraging UE and Quixel assets. 

Hannes Walter, VP of Product Management at Blackshark.AI will demonstrate how Quixel and other Unreal Engine capabilities can further improve the quality of the results. In addition, he will present the most recent results obtained by Blackshark.*
