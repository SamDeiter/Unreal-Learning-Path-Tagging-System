# How Magnopus Brought ‘Fallout’ to Screens with Virtual Production Powered by UE | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which Magnopus discusses the creative and technical process for bringing the dystopian world of ‘Fallout’ to television using in-camera VFX (ICVFX); the real-time flexibility afforded by Unreal Engine; making virtual production work with 35mm film; and much more.
