# Advancing Virtual Production: Exploring the All-AMD Reference Architecture for In-Camera VFX Workflows | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which a panel of industry leaders from AMD, Epic Games, Qube Cinema, Supermicro, Matrox, and Megapixel present an advanced all-AMD reference architecture designed for virtual production in-camera VFX (ICVFX) workflows.  

This innovative system supports the latest video over IP protocols (ST-2110) and integrates seamlessly with Unreal Engine. The panel delves into the advantages of this system, highlighting its affordability and ease of use. 

Check out the video to get insights into the future of virtual production workflows; the potential of this cutting-edge technology; and how to take action to deploy this reference architecture.
