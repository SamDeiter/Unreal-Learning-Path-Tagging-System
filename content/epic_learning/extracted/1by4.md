# HMI for an Industrial Facility Digital Twin with UE5 | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which Implexus Lab presents their innovative development of a digital twin for the Argonne National Laboratory's METL facility. 

This cutting-edge project focused on creating emissions-free electricity at Argonne, leveraging the power of UE5 to monitor and visualize operations in real time. 

The core of this achievement is the seamless integration of Epic MegaGrant-supported tool Datasculpt, which facilitated the connection to live data streams and the generation of dynamic heat map visualizations.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
