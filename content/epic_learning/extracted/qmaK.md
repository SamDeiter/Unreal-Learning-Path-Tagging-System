# Making a Movie in the Cloud: Empowering Collaborative Filmmaking | Unreal Fest 2023

*"Discover the potential of cloud technology in film production as we explore the concept of a Virtual Art Department. This session immerses participants in a space of seamless collaboration, where artists and creative professionals join forces in a virtual workspace, as we review the process of quickly and effectively setting up a Virtual Art Department to bring a captivating horror movie to life. 

Explore the power of cloud-based collaboration and learn how to harness cloud capabilities. You’ll get practical insights and real-world examples of leveraging the Virtual Art Department in the cloud and enabling creators to expand their movie-making endeavors to new heights."*
