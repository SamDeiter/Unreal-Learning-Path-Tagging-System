# Getting the Most out of Epic Games Store and Epic Online Services | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this session, we’ll delve into the opportunities available to you with Epic Games Store and Epic Online Services across both developing your game and bringing it to market.

We’ll begin by recapping the store’s recent progress, including a discussion of self-publishing tools and Epic Rewards. We’ll also provide some insights on best practices when distributing on the Epic Games Store. Additionally, we will explore strategies for crossplay development, which includes the use of Epic Online Services. Finally, we’ll conclude with a peek at the roadmap for the near future before opening up to Q&amp;A.*
