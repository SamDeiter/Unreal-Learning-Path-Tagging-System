- [{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'How does the plugin avoid NetGuid collisions?'}, {'type': 'enhanced_list', 'style': 'unordered', 'items': [[{'type': 'paragraph', 'content': 'The plugin uses RemoteObjectIds to provide objects with a shared ID across proxies, game servers, and clients. However, it is worth noting that our testing of these remote object handles has used internal tech that is not available in the engine, and at this time, we do not have any roadmap to share as to when that functionality may be made available.'}]]}]
