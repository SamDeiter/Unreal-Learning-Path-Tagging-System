# Inside the ‘Yuki-Verse’: Powering an Indie Animation Studio with Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2023

*In this session, find out how indie animation studio Chromosphere went from knowing nothing about Unreal Engine to using it to power the creation of an entire universe around their animated character Yuki 7. You’ll hear from Founder and CEO Kevin Dart, Technical Art Director Theresa Latzko, and Animator &amp; Rigging Artist Lauren Hammond.*
