# Educator Resource Kit - GDC 2023

*Here you will find the resources and materials showcased in the GDC Educator's Summit and extra content, tutorials, blogs and guides developed by Epic Games.
Think of this kit as a “curriculum kickstart;” a guide to help you progress your plans to implement Unreal Engine into the classroom.
Before you dig into the materials, please take a moment to review our hardware and software recommendations if you’re planning on using Unreal Engine at home or in the classroom.*
