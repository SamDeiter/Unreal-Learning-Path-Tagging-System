# Where We’re Going, We Need Roads: The Tech Art of Rocket Racing | Unreal Fest Gold Coast 2024

*Rocket Racing is a supersonic arcade racer that lets you drift, fly, and boost through an ever-growing selection of tracks.

This technical art talk will cover some of the approaches we took to create the roads, obstacles, and dressing that you see as you zoom around the tracks. You’ll be shown lots of fun material tricks and Blueprints that make it easy for environment artists and track authors to create and edit tracks (without having to worry about tweaking material settings).*
