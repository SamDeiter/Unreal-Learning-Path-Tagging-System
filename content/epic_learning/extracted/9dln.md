# Implementing a Quality Cloud CI System Using Horde | Unreal Fest Gold Coast 2024

*In this talk, you’ll get an insight into Riot Games' journey into the implementation and migration to Epic’s Horde CI-CD system with examples of the efforts Riot has been investing in to make Horde the primary CI system for Unreal Engine games, as well of some of the challenges the team has faced.

Riot has focused on providing easy mechanisms for per-team Horde deployments that take advantage of infrastructure-and-configuration-as-code principles, as well as standard infrastructure templates for their commonly used Unreal Engine workflows.*
