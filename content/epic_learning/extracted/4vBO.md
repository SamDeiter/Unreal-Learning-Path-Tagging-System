# How NASA Is Using Simulations and Game Engine Technologies to Help Get Us Back to the Moon | Unreal Fest 2023

*Part one of this session will give an overview of some of the Artemis-focused simulation and visualization systems NASA is building and using at the Johnson Space Center for early analysis and future training for going to the South Pole of the Moon. 

The second part will dive into the details of NASA/JSC’s simulation and visualization applications that are built with Unreal Engine 5, including technical accomplishments and challenges with desktop, video wall, and VR systems.*
