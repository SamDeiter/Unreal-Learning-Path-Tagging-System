# Unreal Fest 2025: Modular MetaHumans & Materials

*"Step into the future of digital creation with MetaHumans! This hands-on, high-energy class shows you how to craft a fully optimized MetaHuman, swap skins like a pro, and effortlessly auto-retarget skeletons to bring your characters to life. 

Discover how to apply multiple motion cycles, attach interactive objects using sockets, and unlock the secrets of animating materials in Sequencer with Material Parameter Constants (MPC). By the end, you'll have a dynamic, swappable MetaHuman with fluid animations, customizable gear, and the tools to take your creations to the next level. Get ready to unleash your creative genius!"*
