# Creating Real-World Interactive Wind Farm Projects for Stakeholder Engagement | Unreal Fest 2024

*This session hosted by engineering consulting firm WSP Canada will focus on the presentation of a cloud-hosted real-world windfarm project used to engage stakeholders and the public. The team will explain how Unreal Engine makes it possible to create 40km by 40km areas with satellite imagery that enables people to see the project from any location. The visualization can be used to review various elements of the real-world project, including proposed layout variants, existing vegetation, day/night for light impact (obstruction lights), differences in volume caused by blade rotation speed, wind direction, seasonal changes, geolocated shadows, and strobing effects on houses.*
