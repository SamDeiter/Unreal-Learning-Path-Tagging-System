# Building Custom Tools and Avoiding Common Pitfalls with Perforce + Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which Jase Lindgren, Sr. Solutions Engineer at Perforce discusses the tips and techniques he used to develop custom tools for Epic’s 4000-person global art jam (Project Titan).  

This video explores how to automate common processes to quickly force unlock files, reset user passwords, create new projects, and much more to keep your game, digital twin, VFX, or animation team collaborating smoothly. 

Jase makes sure you walk away with the skills and mindset needed for building custom tools that run inside P4V and P4Admin to optimize your Unreal Engine development.
