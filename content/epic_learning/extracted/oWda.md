# Current Graphics Considerations in UE5 (UE5.3)

*UE5 includes a host of advanced graphics technologies, such as Nanite, Lumen, VSM, TSR, and more.These are powerful tools but not omnipotent. Each has its own limitations and weaknesses.By understanding these trade-offs, avoiding their weak spots and leveraging their advantages, you can achieve truly high-quality visuals. In this article, we provide an overview of frequently asked questions and concerns. We will continue to revise and expand this content to reflect updates in the latest UE version.*
