# Beyond Average: How Tech Artists Can Make Games More Accessible

*Learn about the context, principles, resources, and actionable ideas for making your games more accessible! This article introduces accessibility concepts and methodologies, along with practical examples of planning and implementing features with accessibility in mind. The content is for anyone interested in creating more inclusive gaming experiences; the technical content is primarily geared towards a technical artist audience.*
