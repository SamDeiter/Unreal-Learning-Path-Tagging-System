# Large-scale Environment Capture for the World's First Hyper-real Wingsuit Simulator | Unreal Fest 2022

*In this panel, we’ll discuss how RealityCapture and Unreal Engine were used to reconstruct an exact 3D digital double of western Utah’s Notch Peak. After months of processing, the result yielded a dataset with a staggering 16 billion polygons, thousands of parts, and thousands of 8K textures.*
