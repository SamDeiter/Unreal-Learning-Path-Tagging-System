# Asset Processing by Quixel

*After processing the images and generating a reconstruction, the resulting high-resolution model and/or texture undergoes Asset Processing. This course details the processes used to produce the final digital asset.*


## Processing Digital Assets

After a 3D model is reconstructed, the model is cleaned up and its size is reduced in a process called decimation. This process produces high resolution and low resolution versions of the model that are retextured using the high quality texture. This course covers the basics of processing the digital asset or assets.

## Purpose

The purpose of this course is to detail the workflow Quixel uses to process digital assets. The methods presented here are not required to digital asset processing unless producing digital assets of MegaScans quality. The process is detailed here as a reference to modify a workflow of your own.

The video below by William Faucher details a more generalized method for asset processing.


## Scope

The scope of this course includes the workflow (or "pipeline") Quixel used for digital asset processing. After scanning, image processing, and model generation, the digital asset receives additional clean up and modification ("decimation") to produce LODs of progressively reduced polygon count.


## Audience

This course is intended for individuals or teams producing high quality digital assets.
