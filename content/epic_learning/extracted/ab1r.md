# Unreal Fest 2025: Introduction to Augmented Reality for Unreal Engine

*Intro to Augmented Reality Lab helps people build their first AR app using Unreal's AR template. This session focuses on the development environment preparation for both Android as well as IOS, comaprision between ARcore and ARKit, a quick dive into AR template to explore the out of box affordance and build custom feature on top of it, and a step by step tutorial of building image tracking from scratch.*
