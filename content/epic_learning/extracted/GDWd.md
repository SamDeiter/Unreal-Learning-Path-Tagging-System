# Epic Secondary Educator Summit- Feb 2024

*On February 24, 2024 we're hosting the first Epic Secondary Educator Summit of 2024. 

An amazing and diverse group of educators will share the great work they’re doing integrating interactive 3D technology into their learning spaces. Interactive 3D content development is providing countless opportunities for students who want to explore new careers in film, games, advertising, architecture, engineering, and more. Throughout the summit, you’ll get to see how your peers are integrating this immersive technology in their curricula. In the process, they’ll help to demystify the tools behind this fast-growing technology, so you can learn how you can bring this technology to your students without the need to be an expert yourself. 

Join us!*
