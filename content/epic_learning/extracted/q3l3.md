# Blur Studio: Crafting an Episode for 'Secret Level' with Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which we delve into how the team at Blur Studio crafted a AAA-quality 15-minute episode for Amazon Prime’s new show, ‘Secret Level’, using their new Unreal Engine pipeline. 

Key contributors walk through the entire process of producing this episode entirely within the engine, covering everything from layout and animation to effects and lighting. 

They explore technical elements such as shot management, lighting passes with Lumen, and the creation of high-density crowds, highlighting their pioneering techniques and collaborative efforts with Epic Games.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
