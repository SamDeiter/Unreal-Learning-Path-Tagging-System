# Project Titan: Building An Open World Game in Public  | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores how Project Titan was made.

Project Titan is a unique proposition: an 8km by 8km scalable, multi-platform open-world sample game built by three Epic developers and populated by 3,000 volunteers over an intense 10-week art jam, with weekly livestreams and a 24/7 Discord. 

Watch this session for a retrospective and demo that covers everything from the underlying UE5 tech powering Titan—including Nanite, Landmass, Mover, and GAS—to the intricacies of having your team size grow from three to 3,000 over a weekend.
