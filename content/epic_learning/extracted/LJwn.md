# Beyond the Bricks: LEGO® Entertainment Goes Real-Time | Unreal Fest 2023

*There are few more trusted or endearing brands for kids and parents alike than the LEGO® brand. Beyond their bricks, the LEGO Group has been capturing audiences' imagination with animated stories from films to TV series. Join this panel as we hear how and why LEGO Entertainment is transitioning several of their most trusted series to real-time pipelines in Unreal Engine and meet the studios that are helping them do it.*
