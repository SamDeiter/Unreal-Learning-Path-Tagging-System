It's chapter 2 and we're one step closer to our shoot day. In this chapter, we'll talk about all the things necessary to prepare for your shoot.


### SHOOT PREP

There are sections on Environment Setup and world creation where we talk about how Epic's ecosystem of products can help you quickly pull together worlds to shoot in and characters to shoot with.

We'll take an in-depth dive into some important things to think about when breaking down your script into a shooting schedule. How to set up a timeline in Sequencer to capture all the capture data you are about to create. As well as some important things to think about and plan for before you start to shoot when it comes to data management.

We'll talk about the advantages of advanced scouting techniques with Unreal Engine's new Virtual Scouting tools and have a long chat about the benefits of Techviz. Especially if you are doing Visualization for any live-action production.


### STAGE TOOLS

Here we'll have a look at building some tools in Unreal Engine using Blueprints that can really help efficiency in your shoot.

We'll learn about the Midi and LiveLink protocols to get information in and out of the engine for use with external gear that you might want on your set.

And we'll look at some plugins that already exist in Unreal Engine that a lot of professionals use during a shoot.
