# Sketchup to Unreal Engine

*In this course from the Epic Online Learning team, you’ll learn how to convert your SketchUp projects to Unreal Engine 5 with ease. We look at how to organize your SketchUp scenes and UV map your objects with the SketchUV plug-in in preparation for export. Import your SketchUp scene using Datasmith for a one-way conversion, or use the Direct Link to update your SketchUp and Unreal Engine scenes at the same time. Explore some built-in lighting options using HDRI backdrops to give your scene realistic lighting. And finally, take a high-resolution image of your scene or create an interactive walkthrough.*


## 📁 Sketchup to Unreal Engine Overview

Take your SketchUp visualizations to the next level using Unreal Engine 5! In this course, you’ll learn how to optimize your SketchUp materials, objects, and scenes for export, then use Datasmith for a straightforward import to Unreal Engine. Still working on your scene? Use Direct the Link to connect SketchUp and Unreal Engine and edit your scenes simultaneously. We’ll also explore ways to light your scene with HDRI Backdrops for easy realistic lighting, and how to create a high-resolution image and an interactive walkthrough to show off your work.
