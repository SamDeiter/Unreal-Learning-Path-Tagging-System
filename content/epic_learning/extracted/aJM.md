# Unreal Engine Essentials for Games | Onboarding Collection

*Welcome to our introductory content collection on Unreal Engine for the games industry, crafted for game developers who are seeking a robust onboarding into the engine.*


## Unreal Engine Essentials for Games

This content collection navigates through the essential aspects of Unreal Engine, encompassing its fundamental engine structure, programming and scripting using C++ and Blueprints. Containing a spectrum ranging from Gameplay, Animation, and AI integration to the complexities of Rendering, World Building, and Physics simulation, participants will acquire a holistic understanding of Unreal Engine's manifold capabilities. The curriculum also explores developer tools and practices, culminating in a comprehensive knowledge of the engine's functionalities.

Note that the course sections are designed to be independent from each other, offering flexibility in the learning journey. Unlike a predefined sequence, there is no prescribed flow or path to follow. Users are encouraged to select topics based on interests and needs, allowing to customize the learning experience.

Please take into consideration that the content is designed for individuals with prior development experience, who are seeking to learn Unreal Engine. If you are completely new to the field of game development, we recommend visiting our Getting Started page.
