# BMW Driving Simulator: Powered by Unreal Engine | Unreal Fest 2024

*BMW has the most advanced and diversified driving simulation center in the world. With over 14 simulators and usability labs, the BMW Group is creating every opportunity for its vehicle research and development engineers to simulate and test future product requirements under realistic conditions. Over the past few years, the automotive giant has converted to Unreal Engine for the simulators’ visualization capabilities. This talk will explore the learnings and highlights from that journey.*
