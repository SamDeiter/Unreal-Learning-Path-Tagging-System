# Rivian UX Program Update: From Early Adoption to Full Lineup | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 in which Rivian and Epic Games give a technical deep dive into the art and development process behind Rivian HMIs. 

We explore how Rivian went from the first UE-powered HMI on the road with the R1T and how they've scaled across their product line. 

The talk also looks at how the automaker has updated their style in Unreal Engine with a cel-shaded theme for the recently released Gen 2 vehicles.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
