# Introduction to Day Sequence | Unreal Fest 2024

*Watch this recorded session from Unreal Fest Seattle 2024 that explores Unreal Engine’s Day Sequence plugin.

Day Sequence provides a Sequencer-driven day / night cycle that provides the flexibility to handcraft a custom look and feel over the course of a day. 

In this session, we uncover the basics of how to enable the plugin; author a simple day sequence that animates the sun to move across the sky using a sample Day Sequence Actor; and go over support for localized volume overrides, procedural actors, and scalability support.

To learn more about Unreal Engine, check out our website: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US*
