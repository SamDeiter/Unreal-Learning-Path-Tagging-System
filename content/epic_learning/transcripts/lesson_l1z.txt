Using our voice to interact with our project provides us a new mechanic to imply intent. It can be a global command that affects everything in the map or a specific command to alter one piece inside of a specific blueprint. In this module, we will prepare both the HoloLens 2 as well as our project to begin listening for our voice. We have spent a lot of time bringing our holograms into our world, but implementing voice into our project is a great way to immerse the end user into the virtual world. We can use our created mechanics to allow a user to navigate a UMG widget using their voice or using their gaze to focus on a specific actor and manipulate it via voice command or broadcast a call to all listening actors through our blueprint interface. Let's get started in a map we've been in before. Open the hand problem map located in world builder maps by course hands hand problem. If you are unfamiliar with this map, we needed to use our hands to both bring out this ramp as well as activate the teleporter. We would then use our gaze to inform our AI guest over here to update the information held by AI friend, letting them know that the treasure they were looking for is over here. What we'll want to do is replicate the solution, but with the addition of voice mechanics. Our added voice interaction is used as an augmented solution and not a replacement. In this module, we prepared our devices to listen to our voice. After opening the project settings, we set up two speech mappings to allow us to interact with blueprints using specific keywords. We used those speech mappings to either communicate directly with one actor that we were focusing on or broadcast to all actors in the scene that we're listening.
