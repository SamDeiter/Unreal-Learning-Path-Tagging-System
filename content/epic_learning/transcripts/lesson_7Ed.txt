This course has really been designed for people who are new to the world of blueprints, and who want to see via some practical examples how they could perhaps start to introduce interactive elements into their visualization projects. Even if you have zero knowledge of blueprints and how they work, you will be able to follow along and benefit from the material that is covered here. However, the course hasn't been designed for people completely new to Unreal itself, although I will show and explain where we are going and what we are doing in the UI as we move forward, I won't be covering things like basic usage of the Unreal Editor and such. If you feel that this could hinder your progress, then please feel free to watch some of the courses in the academy that deal with basic engine usage first of all. After which, you can come back to this course.
