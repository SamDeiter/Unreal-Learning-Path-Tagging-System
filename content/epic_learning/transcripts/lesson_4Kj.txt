Having come to the end of our blueprint examples then, we may be asking what next. Well one thing we can do is try applying the nodes that we've used in the course to any other situations that we can think up as it will help us get to grips with exactly what they can do and where we can use them. For instance, we could try using a line trace to have a door swing open whenever the handle is clicked. We could create a trigger box to turn on automatic lights. And we could use a material swap to show when an electric cooker or other appliance has been turned on. Although I'm sure that you can think of lots of examples yourself that could be used.
