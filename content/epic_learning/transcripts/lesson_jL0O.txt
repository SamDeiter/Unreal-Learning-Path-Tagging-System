Hello, my name is Greg Richardson and I'm the product manager for IK Retargeting in Unreal. IK Retargeting allows for the ability to transfer animations from one character to another, no matter the size, proportions or skeletons of your characters. IK Retargeting has been around since UE5 has launched and has also been recently released on the Unreal Editor for Fortnite, or UEFN for short. In this video series, I'm going to go over the process and detail of how to use IK Retargeting and how you can retarget animations to bipedal characters. For most of the videos in this series, I'll be demoing IK Retargeting in UEFN. However, the same processes and tools that I'm showcasing are the exact same as in UEFN. I'm going to be working in the Animation Starter Project and the UEFN feature examples, which provides a Fortnite mannequin, along with the necessary animation assets that we will need. If you're an Unreal Engine user, similar assets will be provided to you in two ways. You can grab our Content Examples project in the Epic Games Launcher, which also has a logo of the examples for IK Retargeting, or you can create a third-person template project and get the retargeting assets for the Unreal mannequins.
