We have now worked our way through the Hillside sample project. What a wonderful resource we now have that we can refer back to at any time, or even share with others who may be curious about high-quality visual representation using Unreal Engine. There are many gems within Hillside. We simply showed a small portion of the powerful tools in this project. We will leave you with some fun features that you can test yourself by downloading the project. For example, in our Hillside folder, you will see we have included an effects folder. This includes bubblers, coffee steam, falling leaves, and more. A brilliant and easy way to add some vibrant life to your project. You'll find our photogrammetry assets in the levels here. If you'd like to learn more about the capturing and processing of data to create this sample project, check out the learning resources for Habitat 67, which is the name of the built portion of Hillside. Now we have explored the exterior scene as well as the interior scene, and if you were paying attention, you may have noticed a few hidden gems within the populated assets in the scene. If you didn't, take another look, or explore the scene for yourself to see all the thoughtful considerations our developers have taken in creating the sample project. Each of the tools we explored today were included as a way to develop large assets quickly, while also improving efficiency during art direction phases, resulting in a powerful sequence and highly performant real-time experience. With releasing the sample project, our hope is that creators will take these tools into their own workflows to have an improved, efficient experience on their own projects. So feel free to use the modular components of this project and build your own. We'd love to see you share your creations on the unpacked forum or on the Epic Developer Community Portal. To learn more about the topics covered today and additional cinematics rendering and optimization techniques, please refer to the accompanying project sample documentation. Download the project today and have fun exploring the project on your own. We can't wait to see the future work utilizing these tools built by creatives such as you. Thank you for joining.
