Hello and welcome to initial level blockouts and how to start a level for game development. My name is Patrick Haslow and I will be your instructor in this course, the first in a series that will help you become a pro level designer using the Unreal Engine. To give you a bit of my background, I've worked in the AAA game development industry for the past 12 years using Unreal engines. I've spent another 5 years working as a game development instructor, a mentor and author of Unreal Engine tutorials. I have experience in both multiplayer and single player level design on titles such as For Honor, BioShock Infinite and Homefront and others as well. I am currently an advanced level designer at WB Games Montreal on Gotham Knights. In this course you will learn how to plan your level design from 2D to 3D in a level design document. Also we'll learn how to block out using primitive shape static meshes. Then we'll look at balancing detail in a block out to convey designer intentions. And finally we'll look at ways of refining the block out for a good representative gameplay. The target audience for this course is a beginner level designer or junior level designer. Viewer has already installed the Unreal Engine and has taken the Unreal Academy course on UE Editor fundamentals and also taken the introduction to level design for game development course. We assume they have little or no experience creating level blockouts in Unreal Engine and familiarity with basic 2D drawing and 3D modeling apps will be useful to the learner as will a general understanding of basic real world architecture.
