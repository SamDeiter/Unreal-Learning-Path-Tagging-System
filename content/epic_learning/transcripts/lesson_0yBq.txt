Unreal Engine 5 provides a very expansive set of modeling tools that allows you to not only model new assets or adjust existing assets, but you can do this all from within the Unreal Editor 5 UI. Let's pop into the Editor and have a look at this. Now, to access this tool, we come up to our selection mode and down to modeling. Once we've enabled modeling, we have the ability to either add a new primitive, like a box, a sphere, a cone, arrow, a disk, a rectangle, a torus, or a cylinder, or if we have already had a object in the scene and placed and selected when we turned on modeling mode, we could edit the object. Let's go ahead and just click on box and place one down in the scene and press accept. Once I do this, I'm going to have access to many more tools such as X form, which will allow us to do things like adjust the pivot or duplicate or make a pattern out of this. We can also deform, which will allow us to do things like apply maybe a lattice where we can deform this using control points, maybe even sculpt or display or warp it. We can also model by adjusting our poly groups and even do things like cut our object in half using the plane cut underneath mesh. This will allow us to do things like edit our triangles where we can do things like adjust maybe a vertex space or move or rotate objects around within the individual triangles of that particular object. We then have the ability to voxelize or create brand new geometry around an asset and this is really useful if you're working with very high poly assets and you put a bunch together and you want to reduce their complexity without having to go to a third party application. You can then do a voxel wrap, then a voxel merge to wrap your existing geometry and then based up of that wrap produce new geometry that has fewer triangles and fewer materials. We also have the ability to bake things like texture so we can bake normal maps or displacement maps. We can even bake vertex colors right into the vertices of our meshes. We also have the ability to UV objects. Not only can we inspect UVs but we can also relay out existing UVs and even use the built in UV tools to create new UV sets for assets we are making or even create new UV assets for assets that have already been imported. We then have the ability to adjust various attributes as well as under the miscellaneous category here we have the ability to do things like auto-LED, revolve boundary and even convert our mesh to a volume. The modeling tools best part though is that this is all done inside of Unreal Engine 5 without the need to go to another DCC or digital content creation program to get the same results. In fact many of the digital content creation programs that are out there you will find many of the tools that you use already inside of the modeling tools. If you haven't checked them out I highly suggest you do as they are extraordinarily intuitive to use and allow you to create anything you wish right inside of the UE5 viewport.
