Welcome to 3D Map Navigation in VR. My name is Kevin Lyall and I'm your instructor. I'm a technical artist and graphic designer, and I've been working in 3DS Max and Unreal Engine professionally since 2012, creating interactive and experiential projects. In this course, we're going to build a map-based navigation system for teleportation in VR. I've based this course on some of the real world requests I've received from clients, and as a practical example of VR navigation you can use in your own work. This is an intermediate VR course, and I expect you to have a solid understanding of Unreal Engine's interface, as well as a good understanding of Blueprints and UMG. In this first section, I cover the design goals and expectations for our project. Then we will jump right into begin building our navigation system for VR. After we have discussed the goals of our project, we will start by migrating provided assets into our blank project. Then we will begin the development of our base assets, including our player ponds and Blueprint interface. Once we have the pawn working, we will design and build the interactive map to allow us to move around our scene. When the map is ready, we'll take the immersion to the next level by adding VR hands for our player to manipulate. Lastly, we will package our project so that our end users can experience our project in VR. If you're ready, let's get started.
