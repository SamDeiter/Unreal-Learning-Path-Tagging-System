Welcome to developing for the HoloLens 2 in Unreal Engine 4. This is an 11 course learning path designed by Microsoft's HoloLens 2 team and Unreal Online Learning. If you have been following along through the learning path, welcome back. If you would like to follow along from the beginning where we prepare Unreal Engine for mixed reality development, find this learning path on the Unreal Online Learning Course library. This is course 6 where we will prepare our project for speech recognition and voice mapping. In this course, we're going to discover the next layer of interaction for our project. Using voice can not only replace common tasks when we are unable to accomplish them, but also augment them to enable a more dynamic and natural feeling workflow. We'll start in the next module by making sure our microphone is active and Unreal Engine is listening by communicating with some blueprints. Next, we will create some voice inputs to solve a familiar problem, and then we will use what we have learned here and in previous courses to solve a new problem with a broken object in the scene. By the end of this course, we will have a system built that takes advantage of voice and hand interaction, widgets, traditional stereo sound, and spatial sound. The end product will all begin in an increasingly popular mechanic to prepare the situation. We will first say, world builder, and our project will come to life awaiting the next command. Along the way, we will discuss best voice design practices and creating blueprints that are ready for hand and or voice interaction. Let's get started in the next module where we will prepare our devices and find our voice.
