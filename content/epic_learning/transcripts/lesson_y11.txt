Hello and welcome to this course on Building Configurators in UE4. This course will focus heavily on blueprint, a visual scripting language built into Unreal. It is expected that users viewing this tutorial are already familiar with the basics of UE4. You should know how to import assets and know your way around the interface. Please be aware that this course will spend minimal effort making things look good. There are many tutorials out there teaching the necessary skills for visual improvements and you are welcome to apply them as you see fit. This tutorial, as stated, focuses entirely on increasing your skill level in blueprint and will cover basic and advanced concepts alike. So, if you are interested in getting a start or advancing your skills in blueprint, this tutorial is for you. Starting with section 1, we will explore brief and simple examples of each of the required skills you will need. As we progress into section 2, we will begin working our way to a fully functional configurator starting with our key blueprints. In section 3, we will delve into the HUD elements of a configurator and utilize data sheets to feed spreadsheet data to our configurator. In section 4, we will explore a blueprint system for camera switching and control. Lastly, in section 5, we will learn how to implement button sounds and incorporate a simple music player. Upon completion, you will have all the skills required to develop pretty much any kind of configurator to our users. Though I would recommend pushing your way through this entire course from start to finish, you can of course start anywhere. Each part has a corresponding project download which includes the progress of the project to that point. All the downloads include all of the necessary section helpers and should be found in the root of the zip files. For those interested in progressing from start to finish, be aware that you will need to download section 1, part 1 project.zip for the first section of the tutorial, as well as the section 2 part 1 project.zip for all remaining sections and parts. Let's get started.
