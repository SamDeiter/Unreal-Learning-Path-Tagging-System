In this course, we took a look at how Unreal Engine has evolved between versions 4 and 5, and how we can continue working on our existing projects. There's so much more to Unreal Engine 5, we barely scratched the surface, and we plan on bringing you more content covering those new features and systems. So what did we look at? Well here you can see we have our project ported from 4 to 5 running without much issue, and I've also upgraded it to use Lumen for the global illumination system. We have some UI changes that we covered, such as our main toolbar, our docking sidebars, and our content browser changes, along with our bottom status bar. We covered some of our more important engine changes, such as meta sounds becoming the new replacement for our sound system, as well as the introduction of new features such as Lumen and Nanite. In addition to that, there are hundreds of changes and many more new features, such as temporal super resolution, virtual shadow maps, new world building features such as world partition and large world coordinates. We have animation features such as control rig now being a default, along with full-body IK and animation tool scripting. This is just a small bit of the changes and new features in Unreal Engine 5. You can go and read the documentation, as well as check out all the other videos and content that we have created for you.
