As a filmmaker many times during pre-production I want to plan and visualize shots and setups before arriving on set. A lot of questions are asked. What light package is going to give me what I need to achieve my look, feel, exposure, and appropriate depth of field? What camera package and lenses will I need to execute the framing? And how can I be best prepared for blocking using physically accurate camera and light positions? Shotvis is the practice of creating high fidelity still images source from boards are directly from the script. These images are created with physically accurate cameras lights and measurements that match a physical filming set. These techniques can help filmmakers and all storytellers save time and money while they're filming by allowing them to be even more prepared. Shotvis encompasses physically based lighting settings, including physically accurate illumination values. You'll create shotvis to match your vision. Using your storyboards is the basis for your shotvis, creating poses in the animation editor, blocking out your subjects in equipment. You'll then add your physically accurate cameras and your physically accurate lights. And once everything is working in concert together you'll export your final shotvis frames that will serve as a technical document for use on set.
