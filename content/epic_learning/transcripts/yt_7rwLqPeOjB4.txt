Welcome to AnimationHub. Today we're going to look at creating a stylized simple outline material. So, let's get to it. In the content browser, rightclick and create a material. Open the material by doubleclicking the newly created asset and in the material editor, right click on metallic setting and choose promote to parameter. Set the parameter to 1.0. Now right click on the specular setting and select promote to parameter. Set the parameter to 0.0. And finally right click on the roughness setting and select promote to parameter. Set the parameter to 1.0. In the graph area, right click and type camera vector WS and select it to create the node. Next, right click and type pixel normal and select the pixel normal WS node. Dragging from the outpin on the camera vector node, type do fortproduct to create the node. And then dragging from the outpin of the pixel normal worldspace node, connect it to the B pin of the product. Dragging from the outpin on the product node, create a power node. Right click on the exp setting and promote to parameter. Dragging from the outpin on the power node, create a multiply node. Right click on the B setting and promote to parameter. Name it outline intensity. Dragging from the multiply nodes outpin, type subtract and create the subtract node. Right click on its B pin and promote to parameter. Name it outline influence. Drag from the outpin on the subtract node and connect it to both the base color and emissive color settings on the material block. That's it. To see the result, save the material and close the graph. In the content browser, right click on the material and select create material instance. Drag the material instance onto a mesh in your level. Doubleclick the material instance to adjust the parameters of the material to your liking. I hope this was helpful. See you next time.
