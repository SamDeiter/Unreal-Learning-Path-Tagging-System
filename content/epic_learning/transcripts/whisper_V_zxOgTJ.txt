I use this method because it lets me control the density of the forest on a less granular level. I'm not worrying about individual trees, but rather how I use groups of them. I can cluster together copses to create denser areas of trees, knowing that I'll get denser undergrowth to go with it. I can also spread them out and cull extra elements to create the sparser forest edge, causing it to naturally recede into the landscape instead of being a hard-ish line. In my case I do this as a separate PCG graph, and then have it double as natural looking sparsely wooded terrain elsewhere.
