All the objects in our scene, the meshes and lights and so on, go through multiple layers every frame to create the image we see, and at the end is post-processing. The effects we see are generally in what's known as screen space, which means that all that is affected is what we currently see on the screen. This is so we can add effects relative to where our camera is, such as lens flares and bloom effects. We can also use post-processing to do things like color grading and tone mapping.
