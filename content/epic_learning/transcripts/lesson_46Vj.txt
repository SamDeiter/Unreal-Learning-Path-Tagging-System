It's important that we understand the roles of all participants in order to understand why and how UI is typically used in level design. So the role of the level designer, they design players gameplay experience. They decide when to convey level design or narrative intentions in the UI. They decide when to guide the player's movement through the space in UI. So a UI designer, designs how informational will be shown to the player. Students can be aesthetic or animation. They design the aesthetics of categories of information shown to the player. They also design interactions with the UI. Button presses, controller, use, that kind of thing. The UI programmer, they create the systemic functionality of the UI. They create a scriptible functionality of the UI, essentially giving tools to the designer to use in creating a player experience. And they support the design needs of the UI designer and the level designer. Let's take another look at how iteration and collaboration can take place between the members of these different teams.
