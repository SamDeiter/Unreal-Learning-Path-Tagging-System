Unreal Engine 5's foliage mode allows you to paint massive expanse of foliage across landscapes, static meshes, BSPs, even on fully existing foliage meshes and on translucent surfaces. You can use this to interactively paint, move, and erase various static meshes that are going to make up foliage or trees, bushes, and rocks in your project. Let's look at how we use this inside of the editor. Now to access foliage mode, we come over here to our selection and then we go down to foliage. Once we have the foliage mode activated, all we need to do then is find some static meshes and drag and drop them to where it says drop foliage here. Once we have those added, we can simply click on them and then start to paint. If I hold down shift while holding down the left mouse button, I will erase. And if I hold down just the left mouse button, you can see here I am applying those to the surface. If I was to add more foliage, types to their different static meshes, you can see here I can paint and remove them. If I come here to select, I can also select individual assets and I can move them, I can rotate them and scale them as well. When real engine fives foliage tools also work with the landscape tools, and you can even have foliage be restricted or only be placed when certain landscape layers are painted.
