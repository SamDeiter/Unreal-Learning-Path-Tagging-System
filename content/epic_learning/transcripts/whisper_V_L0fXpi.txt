It's not unusual to see a game leverage 2-3 rock meshes for an entire environment. The next question is, how do we detail it all out in the time we have? A city without props and clutter is going to feel like an empty film set. A forest without trees or undergrowth and deadfall isn't even that. Consider using PCG for prop placement, rather than doing it all by hand. You'll thank yourself later, especially if you're using variants of this layout in multiple locations.
