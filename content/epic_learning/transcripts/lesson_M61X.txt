Material instances are used to change the appearance of the material without incurring an inexpensive recompilation of the material. A typical material cannot be changed without recompiling, which you don't want to do during gameplay, whereas a parameterized material can be edited in the material instance without the recompilation. It has numerous workflow advantages and can improve material performance.
