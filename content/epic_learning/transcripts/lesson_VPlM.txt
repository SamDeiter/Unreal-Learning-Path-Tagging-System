We've gone through the basics of the engine. We've looked at the editor, we've brought in content from various places, created our own level, set up some visual controls such as adjusting our lights, and we touched on the basics of blueprints. We've even packaged our project to share with others. At this point I would suggest continuing doing what we've been doing by experimenting with the project you have opened now. Redesign the level using the assets given to you. Give your player a place to run around or even through. Add more props or obstacles to get into their way and interact with them. Change the lighting, give it a more wacky feeling. This project is now your playground, so start playing with it and have fun. Once you're ready to move on, I'd recommend going to the sample section on the launcher. Here you can find new and old content that you can look at to get a feeling of other features available to you in the engine. In particular though, I would go to the content example sample. This is a great resource full of sample maps covering the majority of the features available to you inside the engine. It's a great way to learn things such as landscape or even math. After this I'd recommend going to the EDC or the Epic Developer Community site. This is where you can find forums as well as learning material from our library. Interact with other community members in the forums. Go ahead and go to the learning section and you can look at the library where we can find Epic Games content as well as community content. For example if you're looking at what to do next, you can go ahead and go through and look at all the other topics available to you. Next I'd recommend going to the documentation page. This is a great place to not only get started but find more information on what is available to you inside the engine. As you can see here, there's other great places to work on understanding the basics, working with content or even specific things such as programming and scripting or animating characters and objects. Additionally, you will find other courses linked below from the Epic Online Learning team. Unreal Engine is a tool that you can use to build anything you can think of with enough practice and learning and while it will take some time and effort, there's plenty of learning resources available and you will soon be able to bring your creativity to life.
