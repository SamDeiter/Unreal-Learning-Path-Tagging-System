Color grading and the filmic tone mapper can be used at runtime to control the tonality of the scene or create any style including black and white, sepia-toned film looks, highly saturated styles and more. Unreal renders everything in high dynamic range. The tone mapper then maps HDR color into low dynamic range color so that a typical monitor can display the HDR effects including things like correct emissive lighting and exposure as accurately as possible. Unreal uses a filmic tone mapper based off the Academy color encoding system or aces. Which is regarded as industry standard for film and TV. Aces provides access to a color space that is the closest approximation of the entire color gamut available to the human eye. Let's look at some of these settings for a moment.
