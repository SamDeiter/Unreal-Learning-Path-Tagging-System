Welcome to using Magic Leap. My name is Alex Coulomb and I'm excited to showcase some of the ways we can utilize Magic Leap's first device, the Magic Leap One, with Unreal Engine. The goal of this course is to introduce the development pipeline for Magic Leap One on Unreal Engine while drawing special attention to the device's features through an engaging and practical use case, an architectural site model. We will view, manipulate, and interact with this scaled virtual building in an augmented reality, real world environment, in ways that go above and beyond the possibilities of a physical site model. The course will progress as follows. One, to prepare our development pipeline, we will become a Magic Leap Creator on Magic Leap's website, download the Magic Leap Package Manager, and install all necessary SDKs and tools. Two, because our site model is in Revit, we'll take advantage of Unreal Status Myth plugin to bring all of our 3D assets and materials into Unreal Engine 4.22. Three, to understand the scale of our model in the context of a real world environment, we'll view the model in the Magic Leap Simulator in a virtual room, then through an actual Magic Leap One device. Four, every real world environment is different, so we'll appraise three methods of locating our site model. First, using our controller's touchpad, then as a full attachment to the controller, and lastly, snapping the model to a real world image target. Five, to give a sense of being better nested into its surroundings, we'll set up our Magic Leap One to scan the environment to include our virtual model with real world objects, as well as modify the hard edges of our Magic Leap One's field of view with a softer vignette. Six, Magic Leap One recognizes hand gestures, which is great for anyone who wants to be very hands-on with digital content, and not at the mercy of a peripheral. We'll end our course by learning how to use gestures to swap out design options. I welcome you to follow along with the course as we develop an understanding of how best to harness the capabilities of the Magic Leap One with all the power of Unreal Engine behind it.
