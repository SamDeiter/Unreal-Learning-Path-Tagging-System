Hello and welcome to Implementing Level Design with Blueprints. My name is Patrick Haslow, and I'll be your instructor in this course. The next in the series that will help you become a pro-level designer using the Unreal Engine. My background includes 12 years in AAA game development using Unreal engines. I spend five years as a game development instructor, mentor, and author of Unreal Engine tutorials. Among other titles I have experienced in multiplayer and single-player level design, on For Honor, Bioshock Infinite, and Homefront. I'm currently an advanced level designer at WB Games Montreal on Gotham Knights. What you will learn in this course includes planning gameplay within a space to define the scope of the player experience, integrate gameplay with interactive actors and blueprint scripting in a level block out, implement different methods of moving level geometry, implement communication between actors in the world and the level blueprint, create player progression logic in the level blueprint, create scripted events in the level blueprint. The target audience for this course is that the intended viewer as a beginner level designer or junior level designer, the viewer has already installed the Unreal Engine and has taken the following courses, Unreal Academy course on UE Editor Fundamentals, exploring level design for game development, creating a level block out for game development, and Blueprints and gameplay for game designers. We assume that they have little or no experience scripting gameplay using Blueprints in Unreal Engine. By the end of this course, you should be able to script a series of gameplay experiences using Blueprint in a map level that can be played by others for a fun progression through the playable space.
