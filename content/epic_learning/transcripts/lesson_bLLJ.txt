Because light domes are critical to the success of this type of lighting method, it is very helpful to have a resource where you can produce many different scenes easily. I generally like to mock up my scene first, even when I know I'm going to photograph my own. Before to check out HydriHaven.com as they have many nice skydomes to choose from, and they're 100% free. You may need to convert them from HDR to EXR format before importing, but a tool like Photoshop makes quick work of that task. Once in a suitable format, you can just drag it into your project and it will be ready for use.
