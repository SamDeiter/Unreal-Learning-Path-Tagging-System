Now that our project has finished opening and upgrading to Unreal Engine 5, let's take a quick look at the user interface changes. The first thing you may notice is we have a new darker look and say flat look. One thing you might immediately notice missing is our place actors, where we have quick access to all of our items is no longer there on the left. That is now accessed through our Create menu. On our top main toolbar, we click the drop down with the plus and this says Quick Access to Creating Items. So anything we could create before from our place actors panel can now be placed in here. Or we can click on Place Actors panel to bring it back. And it will dock by default on the left-hand side. One nice addition is Sidebars. While the place actors panel is nice to have open, sometimes we don't need it, we can right click on any of our panels, choose Doc to Sidebar, and it will now be placed along the side. We can go ahead and click on any of our panels and they will pop out once they are docked to the sidebar. And when we are done using them, if we click Black in the Viewport, they will go ahead and hide. We can have multiple panels docked along the sidebar. In addition to the left sidebar, there is a right sidebar. I can right click on the Levels, Doc to Sidebar. Since this panel was near the right side, it will automatically dock it to the right panel. If you want to bring something back, you can right click on it and un-dock it. One thing you could do is pin your sideboards open. If you click the Pin tab, they will not pop open and stay over anything you might be using. When you are done, you can unpin them and they will go back to the normal Doc method. On the bottom, we have some changes. We have a Content Drawer. I will cover this in more detail in the next module, but if you click on it, it is a pop-up or a bottom sidebar version of our Content Browser. We also have the Output Log, always available from the bottom as a pop-up, and we have an always access command bar at the bottom. On the bottom right, we have our Drive Data Cash information, as well as our Source Control. Source Control is no longer button at the top. This is where you will access it to connect and do all of your Source Control changes. As you can see, the majority of the interface is functionally the same. It just looks different. One thing that might throw you for loop though is the Windows that are docked are now docked below the main menu bar. In previous versions of Unreal, if you were to have a window that was floating and went to dock it, you could dock it on the top of the screen. You'll notice this no longer works because it's docking below the menu bar now. If you're looking at a few of the common parts of the UI of this changed, let's look at something that has had more significant changes, and that is the main toolbar which we'll look at next.
