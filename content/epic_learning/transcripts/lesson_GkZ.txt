Getting a good mix for your game is probably one of the most challenging things in game audio. You've got to balance the competing demands of potentially hundreds of different sounds, with identifying the most important audio for a player to hear at any given time. This means that your mix needs to be dynamic, and needs to reflect not only the typical considerations like volume or frequency ranges of the sounds, but also the number of specific sound types that you're going to allow to be played at any given time, and what the priorities are when there's just too much going on. Effects give us the opportunity to really sculpt, shape and transform sounds in real time. Their crucial to getting the audio to respond to events within games, and to creating a controlled and polished mix. Having worked your way through the course, you'll now be able to apply these newfound skills to your audio to transform the sound of your game, and the way in which you can respond to gameplay.
