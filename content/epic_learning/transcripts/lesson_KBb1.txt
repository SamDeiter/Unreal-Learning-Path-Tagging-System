Although there is no real need for prior knowledge of Unreal's lighting tools while working through this course, I will be assuming that you at least know the basics of the Unreal UI and that you understand how to navigate through scenes and perform typical viewport operations. A basic understanding of how light works in the real world will be helpful in terms of putting the tools that we will look at and discuss to good use in Unreal. But again, there is no specific need for this knowledge in order for you to follow along with and benefit from this particular course.
