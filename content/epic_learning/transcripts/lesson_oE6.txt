A spotlight is a light that emits from a single point in a cone shape. There are two cone shapes that shape how the light works. The inter cone where the light achieves full brightness relative to its intensity settings and the outer cone where the light intensity falls off or diminishes the further it gets from the source. This light is good for flashlights or stage lights where lighting is being directed from a source or funneled in a specific direction. This is the newest addition to the lighting types and it emits light in the scene from a rectangular plane with a defined width and height. This light is used to simulate the shape of a rectangular light emitter compared to a point or spotlight which are more curved or rounded in nature.
