Hello, my name is Simon Blakeney, I'm a technical accounts manager at Epic Games, and the primary part of my role at Epic is to help customers with the technical adoption of the Epic ecosystem. The Epic ecosystem contains a range of powerful tools that are well suited to LIDAR and FOGGRIMITRY pipelines. Animating LIDAR datasets can often be viewed as a tricky task given the scale of some surveys. However, with Unreal Engine, we are able to unleash the immense, built-in graphical capabilities that the engine offers in combination with the versatile UE point cloud plugin. We can visualize and add interactivity mechanisms to point clouds with billions of points. Photogrammetry assets are commonplace in Unreal Engine scenes, and with Nanite Virtualized Geometry in Unreal Engine 5, we are no longer constrained by photogrammetry polycounts, draw calls and mesh memory usage. To restore LIDAR scans were captured in the early phases of Project Hillside, then fed into Reality Capture alongside drone and ground derived photography. Reality Capture enabled the team to create accurate photogrammetry and registered LIDAR point cloud datasets that could then be used as reference when building the reimagining of Habitat 67. It's worth noting at this point that the captured data isn't perfect. It has faults, but it gives us an opportunity to run through issues that might be encountered with your own LIDAR and FOGGRIMITRY projects.
