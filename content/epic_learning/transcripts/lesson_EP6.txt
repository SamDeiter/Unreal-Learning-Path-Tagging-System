Hello, and welcome to Introduction to Level Design for Game Development. My name is Patrick Haslow. I will be your instructor in this course. The first in a series that will help you become a pro-level designer using the Unreal Engine. To give you a bit of my background, I've worked in the AAA game development industry for the past 12 years using Unreal Engines. I've spent another 5 years working as a game development instructor, a mentor, and author of Unreal Engine Tutorials. I have experience in both multiplayer and single-player level design on titles such as For Honor, BioShock Infinite, and Homefront, and others as well. I am currently an advanced level designer at WB Games Montreal on Gotham Knights. What you will learn in this course includes setting up a project for level design, using level design editor tools and applying best practices, using developer materials for scale and conveying intention, creating and editing geometry brushes, and using geometry brushes to export aesthetic mesh proxies for level blockouts. Our target audience for this course is that the intended viewer is a beginner level designer or junior level designer, and the viewer has already installed the Unreal Engine and has taken the Unreal Academy course on UE Editor fundamentals. We assume that they have little or no experience with creating level blockouts in Unreal Engine. For this course, familiar already with basic 2D drawing and 3D modeling apps will be useful to the learner as will a general understanding of basic real world architecture. By the end of this course, you should be able to create a blockout that is completely playable by others and is a fun representative first pass of proposed gameplay spaces described in the level design.
