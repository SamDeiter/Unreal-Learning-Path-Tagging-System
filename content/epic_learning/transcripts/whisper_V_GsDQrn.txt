Hi folks, Sam here. Wanted to use this nifty layout in the software region to talk a little bit about game camera. Basically, these huts fit our in-game third person camera perfectly, horizontally and vertically. You can see it push in a little, because I'm off-centre in the room. What that means is that any space smaller than this will push in the third person camera, and that includes rooms the size that are prop heavy. Not to say that you can't make a room smaller or denser than this, but you should absolutely playtest it at the blockout phase before committing to a scale. See you next time.
