Hi, and welcome to Advanced PBR Materials. My name is Matthew Doyle, and I'll be your guide. This course serves as a second part to my course on Introduction to Materials for Design Visualization. In this course, we'll cover some of the core concepts for delving deeper into the Physically-Based Material System in Unreal Engine. We'll look at topics such as creating material instances for improving the material editing workflow. Working with parameter collections, which allow us to alter multiple materials at one time, and building our own custom material functions. We'll also take a closer look at working with the various shading models, including using clear-code shading to mimic multi-layer materials, which have a translucent paint or film, as well as simulating fuzzy cloth surfaces and translucent two-sided foliage. In the third part of the course, we'll examine several example materials commonly needed when building real-time architectural scenes, including tinted glass surfaces, surfaces such as shaggy rugs and fur using displacement mapping, and we'll look at how to animate objects such as curtains that blow in the wind using materials and vertex colors. Before we get started, let's discuss some of the things you should know and some of the software and assets you'll need for the course.
