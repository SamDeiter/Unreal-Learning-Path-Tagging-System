We've reached the end and I hope you have found this material essentials concept course interesting. During this course we've gone through everything related to the essentials of working with the material editor of Unreal Engine 4. We've made different kinds of materials using features such as world position offset to drive the animation for curtain, taken a look at how to alter pbr textures and materials and how to work with translucent and massed materials. What we've covered in this course concerned only the material editor itself, but the Unreal Engine material tool set is made up of a lot more tools and features than only the material editor. And that's something we will dive into in the course on material workflow where we will further expand on these tools and how the material editor fits into the bigger picture.
