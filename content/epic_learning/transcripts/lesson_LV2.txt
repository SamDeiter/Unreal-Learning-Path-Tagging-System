Welcome to the course Optimizing for Real Time. My name is Steve Began, and in this course, you will learn about the importance for optimization in an Unreal Engine project. We'll identify a few ways to profile how efficiently we are rendering models and materials in the viewport, then we'll look at how we can optimize various elements of a project to improve performance. Unreal Engine projects do not always perform in most optimal way by default. As you develop your program or your environment, you should pay attention to how well it will run on the target hardware. A well-optimized project is also extensible and ultimately designed to perform well for the end user. Optimization isn't just, does my experience run at an extremely high frame rate? It's a balance between performance and quality. In the best case scenario, your project will have the highest possible quality while also hitting your performance targets. At times, modifying your project to meet your goals can be like trying to hit a moving target. As you use functions and tools to get back performance, you have more room to enable expensive features, which then require more optimization and so on. Optimizing an Unreal Engine project can take a lot of time, but it's a very important step in project development. Next, we'll look at what it means to set a performance target and identify some key metrics for performance.
