We need characters. Characters are the engine that drive story. A character allows us to identify archetypes that are familiar to us, and then tune those archetypes into specific and unique experiences that propel action. The most universal way in which we create story is when we tell our friends and loved ones about our day. We tell them about what we saw, experienced, the emotions we felt when certain things happened to us. These stories rely on the fashioning of the self as a character. I'm Rocky Sayid, I'm a writer and tech artist, and I teach here at Teharengo Waka in Wellington, New Zealand. Welcome to Real Virtual Beauty Training, supported by Unreal Engine Education, the Center for Appearance Research, and the Dove Self-Esteem Project. I'll be your guide on this journey. What does Real Virtual Beauty mean to you? In this course, you will learn about the principles of design, and how these principles or methods can help us simplify technical art pipelines for creating digital characters. We'll look closely at key areas of character design, such as faces, skin, hair, costume, lighting, and materials. We'll break down these complex topics by concentrating on storytelling that centers characters, specifically diverse, authentic, and female identifying characters.
