Over this course, we've covered a lot of ground and discovered a variety of tools to help us stylize our renders. We discussed the best practices of setting up a project when desiring a specific outcome. We discovered how to flatten our render by overlaying the base color onto the scene. We examined how to implement outlines on our entire level and on select objects. We crunched the lighting to get a high contrast graphic effect. We also built procedural gradients to add banding and color to the lights. Lastly, we created a paint-fully effect to render on the level. By blending these techniques together, we saw a demonstration of a variety of results. The techniques used in this course are only a few that can be implemented to generate post-process materials. Now that we have these tools, they should provide a launching point for further exploring and learning how to customize your own render style.
