There are three lighting types, static, stationary, and movable. Static lighting is fully baked lighting. It does not affect any movable objects nor can the light itself be moved. This light has what I would call medium-quality settings, but it also has the lowest performance impact on the scene. Stationery lights are also baked lighting, but they are better quality than static lighting. So this is the type of light you'll generally want to use when baking your light maps. Stationery lights can also change color and brightness at runtime, but like static lights, they cannot be moved. They store indirect light and shadow in light maps. Direct shadows are stored in separate shadow maps. Stationery lights can also project dynamic shadows on movable objects, and it uses distance field shadows which are very high quality for this.
