Hello, my name is Matthew Wadstein and welcome to Lumen Essentials. So what is Lumen? Lumen is a new, full-ed dynamic global illumination and reflection solution that immediately reacts to scene and light changes, offering artists and designers the ability to create more dynamic scenes with greater realism. Change the sun angle for time of day, turn on a flashlight, open an exterior door, or even blow up a ceiling and indirect lighting and reflections adapt accordingly. The system renders diffuse into reflection with infinite bounces and indirect specular reflections in huge detailed environments at scales ranging from kilometers to millimeters. So what are these features of Lumen? We have robust dynamic global illumination. Sky lighting is solved as part of Lumen's final gather. This includes sky shadowing, which allows indoor spaces to be much darker than outdoor. It provides lower quality, global illumination for lit translucency and volumetric fog. Emissive materials can now propagate the light through Lumen's final gather with no additional performance costs. And Lumen also solves for us indirect specular or reflections for the full range of material roughness values.
