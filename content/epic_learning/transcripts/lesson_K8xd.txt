We've learnt how to import, clean, animate and render LiDAR point cloud files in Unreal Engine. We've also learnt how to import, clean and separate photogrammetry measures. And what could we do next with this data and these techniques? Well, we could experiment with different materials and material parameter collections to achieve alternative, animatable effects. We could mix together LiDAR and photogrammetry assets in a single scene. We could create modular photogrammetry building blocks for game design and UEFN projects. We could explore these LiDAR and photogrammetry files in virtual reality. We could couple these scenes with pixel streaming technology to deliver the content to web browsers. We could create digital replicas of film sets, retrofit designs, sculptures and more. And we could add interactivity to those assets, linking them with open source data sets via rest APIs and other game mechanics. The possibilities are endless and we look forward to seeing what you do next with the Epic ecosystem.
