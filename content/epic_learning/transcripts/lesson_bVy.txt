Hello and welcome. My name is Shorthion and in this series of classes we will be taking an in-depth look into the material tools and features of Unmaging 4. The goal is to give an overview of all the different ways one can work with materials in the engine and how all those different tools and features impact the workflow, when to use which one and what settings or considerations to take into account. We will start this series with an introduction to the general behind the scenes architexure, the concept of physical base rendering and how we work with textures. After that we will start out by diving into the material editor itself and its material expressions and common combinations of those expressions. Followed by taking a look at the various material inputs, shading models, blend modes and material domains. And then once we've gone through the material editor itself we will look into material instances, functions, layers, material parameter collections and HLSL which are all of the different tools you have available for working with shaders and materials. And finally we will take a look at how to combine the different tools together and we will wrap all of this up by going through a couple of advanced examples.
