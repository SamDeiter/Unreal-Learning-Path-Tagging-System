From a workflow perspective, they can be thought of as the most basic tool for level construction and unreal. Geometry brushes add or subtract volumes of space in the level, often used in combination to create Boolean-style subtractions of negative space from positive space. We could use geometry brushes for rapid prototyping, for simple filler geometry, to include spaces that fill in the gaps between static meshes in a playable space. The ineditrification of geometry brushes allows easy maintenance of important scale relationships in the world. They also serve as a convenient method of creating proxy static meshes. Although the use of geometry brushes in AAA development is rare, we will be using them here to illustrate important level design pre-production concepts.
