In the content browser, navigate to LearningKit Games, Material Library and we can go into the Object-Based Gradients directory. Browse the available assets and left click once on, say, this one, the Gradient Seramic Material to make it the active selection. With that ready, click on your SM underscore game fan in the viewport to select it. Look over to the detail panel on the right side of the screen, scroll down until you find a material section and locate the element zero material slot. All you have to do now is click this small you selected asset from content browser arrow icon. And just like that, the material is instantly applied. So applying pre-existing materials is incredibly fast and powerful. But what happens when you want to create a specific look that you can't find in a library?
