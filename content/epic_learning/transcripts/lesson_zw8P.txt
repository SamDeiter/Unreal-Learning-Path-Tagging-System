Hello and welcome. My name is Paul Kind and I am an Unreal Engine expert. In this tutorial series I'm going to share with you some tips, tricks and techniques for making your next outdoor automotive scene come to life. To begin we will start by assembling a very simple but highly practical scene. We will then import high dynamic range images and assemble them into a skydome. This skydome will be responsible for both our backdrop and our lighting. Finally we will import our subject matter in our case a motorcycle and prepare it for light baking. We will then go through all the required steps to hone our final image into something epic. By the end you will have all the knowledge needed to handle outdoor scene lighting for automotive shots.
