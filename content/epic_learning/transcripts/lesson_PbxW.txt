The Unreal Engine Editor is how we work with Unreal Engine and our projects. It allows us to take our ideas and turn them into what we want. By getting a good grasp on how we can work with the Editor, it will help speed up our development. In this course, we started off by looking at the Unreal Engine's default layout and how we would work with setting up the layout to meet our needs for when we're working in our project. We looked at the different parts of the Editor, such as our Viewport window, our Outliner panel, the Details panel, and the Content Browser. The main toolbar gave us access to the different modes as well as our settings. In addition to additional preferences for the Editor and our project settings, lastly, we wrapped it up by taking a quick look at the World Settings panel and what it can do for us.
