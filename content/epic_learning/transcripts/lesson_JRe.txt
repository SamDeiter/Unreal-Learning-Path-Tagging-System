What we have covered in this material workflow course are the different additional material tools and features that you have available in the Unreal Engine to support the material editor. We have gone through what material instances are and how they make it easy to adjust, manage and collaborate on a large material library. We have looked at the use and power of material functions and how they greatly speed up the workflow and collaboration by grouping common or complex combinations of expressions into functions. We also went through material layers and how they use a layer-based workflow in the material editor. And we have taken a look at material parameter collections and how they upgrade for altering values in great many materials at once. For example, for driving the strength of wind across all foliage in the environment.
