We will be deploying a project for playback on a website. Because of this, it will be important to test on an appropriate browser. The two browsers I would recommend, especially for initial testing, are Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox. Be sure to have them fully updated and using 64-bit version if your computer allows. If you're using Chrome, you can check for 64-bit by selecting the triple dots to the top right of the browser bar. Then selecting Help, then about Google Chrome. It will then list your version number and your build type. Just check to see if it's 64. In Firefox, you can check your version by clicking the triple lines of the far right of the address bar and selecting Help, and then again about Firefox. To reiterate, in either Chrome or Firefox, it is going to be preferred that you have the 64-bit version and not the X86 version. Let's get started.
