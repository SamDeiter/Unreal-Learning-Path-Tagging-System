Assets are a fundamental part of working with Unreal Engine. They make up the core of your project and keep them under control. Organized and efficient is always something to keep in mind as you are working. We took a look at the basic ways of importing assets into Unreal Engine, getting them in and the different import options we may have available to us. We then looked at the basics of asset management, such as creating them, deleting them, and the concept of redirectors. We touched upon migrating assets from one project to another, as well as using the ability to auto-reimport assets that may have been edited outside without having to use the manual import process. We then finished up by looking at a few of the different tools available to us in the editor, such as the reference viewer, the property matrix editor, and the size map. There are additional resources available to you. I would recommend going to the documentation page if you'd like to find out more. We have some nice asset and content pack pages that give you more details on the information we covered today, as well as additional things such as working with asset-mated data and consolidating assets. I really hope you enjoyed this course and took away some useful information on how to work with assets inside of the Unreal Editor.
