In this module we've learnt how to create groups of pulses using the optional pulse override in order to implement music that uses complex or irregular time signatures. There are many reasons why you might choose to use these kind of time signatures for dynamic music but a particularly effective time at which to use them might be in what could be referred to as pre-end periods. These are periods when you know that a significant event or change in the game is very likely. Using more complex or ambiguous music in these periods helps to break any structural patterns of expectation in the music and so makes it more able to transition quickly in response to the event.
