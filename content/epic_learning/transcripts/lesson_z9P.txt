Welcome, my name is Shurt Young and in this Material workflow course we are continuing our dive into how to author and work with materials in on 1984. After we covered the essentials of the Material Editor itself, in this course we will focus on the additional tools and features that surround the Material Editor and how to have a major impact on workflow and content pipeline. Things like material instances, material layers, material functions, and material parameter collections all play a supportive role towards the Material Editor and they greatly expand the power and flexibility of the Material System in the engine. It is thus vital that you have a grasp of what these additional tools do and how to let them empower you in your work.
