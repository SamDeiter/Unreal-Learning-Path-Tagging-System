During the course, we'll get acquainted with a very useful set of tools within Unreal. First off, we'll use actor blueprints to create our own customizable characters. Actor blueprints are a great way to add diverse, easy to customize assets to your projects. Next up, we'll dive into animation blueprints and setup our characters for live motion capture data transfer. Animation blueprints are also great for editing animations that run time, which I'll demonstrate. After that, comes the backbone of the pipeline, the live link window, where we'll establish performance streaming between our 3D applications and Unreal. With all that setup complete, we'll jump over to the take recorder, where we'll record several live motion capture performances while also touching upon production usage cases. During this process, we'll also use sequencer, where we'll review our performances. Sequencer is the cinematic and cutscene toolkit of Unreal, where you can edit your recordings however you like and do so much more.
