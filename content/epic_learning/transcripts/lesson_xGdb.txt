The goal of this course is to build a strong, foundational 2D project, dynamic lighting, enemy characters and player camera control. We'll begin by modifying the base Paper 2D material to support dynamic lighting. While moving on to tweaking our project settings for optimal rendering results. We'll then improve the player camera by allowing to recontrol over this camera relative to the player character's position. Next, we'll create an AI character that can sensibly traverse the environment. When enhanced, this character by adding the ability to send and receive damage between the player and the enemy character. This course assumes that you are somewhat familiar with the basics of Blueprint in Unreal If that is not the case, I suggest taking West Bund's Blueprint's Essential Concepts course beforehand. You may also find it helpful to take the Getting Started with your Paper 2D project course for a deeper understanding of the starter project that is provided. However, this is not required. By the end of the course, you will have built a 2D environment that is dynamically lit with a moving enemy character that can send and receive damage to the player.
