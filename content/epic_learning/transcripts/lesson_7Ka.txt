The ability to use audio analysis to drive in-game parameters or instigate events is probably one of the most exciting opportunities that Unreal Engine has developed in recent years, and there's lots of creative work and applications to be explored here. Each approach to audio analysis has its own pros and cons, but the systems within the synesthesia plugin for detecting loudness, pitch and audio on sets have huge potential that we've only briefly touched upon. For real time analysis, the ability to detect amplitudes within an audio component allows us for the first time to respond to how the sound of audio changes when real time effects are applied, and the ability to interrogate the amplitude within specific frequency bands allows us to tie aspects of the game to musical pitch or rhythm. Finally, the fact that we can do this in real time on any sub mix affords us the opportunity to apply this to a player's microphone input, another technique that's rich with possibility. Through this course you'll have learnt to apply these different techniques, and now hopefully you can take them in new creative directions.
