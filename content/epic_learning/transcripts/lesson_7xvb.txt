The content examples project is a project that is provided by Epic Games that contains a large amount of example maps and scenes and assets that we can use to learn from. I know personally when going through schooling, I didn't focus heavily on math because I didn't find a need for it. Of course I was mistaken later when I tried to start developing games and learned math is everywhere. Going through this example map is a great way to brush up on your math skills and pick up on things that you may not already know. We can get to this content examples by going to the Learn tab on the launcher, scrolling down to content examples, and then we can go ahead and create a new project. You'll notice the supported intro versions go back to 4.0, up to the current 4.26. This content example project is actually updated every time a new version comes out and they give us examples for new features. On the left hand side of the math hall are visual representations of each of these math functions, and on the right hand side are interactive examples showcasing how we might use these inside of the engine. There are two different sections. The first one deals with basic math such as maybe how cosine and sine works and dealing with fractional numbers. And our second section deals with more complex vector math such as maybe how the dot or the cross product functions work.
