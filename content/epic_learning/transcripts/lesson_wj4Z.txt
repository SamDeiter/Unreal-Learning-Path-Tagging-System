Unreal Engine 5 is a very powerful set of tools for managing any type of vast open-world expanse you might want to create for your project. These tools called Landscape Mode allow you to create landscapes of varying height and size. Let's take a look at these tools inside of the editor. To access the landscape tools, we first come up to our selection mode and then select landscape. From here we have the ability to manage the sculpt or paint our landscape. However, sculpt and paint will not be available until we actually have a landscape in our level. Let's go ahead and create one by just pressing the blue create button right here. Now that we have this inside of our level, if we come to the sculpt tool, which will automatically default tool, we can then hold the left mouse button and drag around the level viewport in order to sculpt our landscape. Different tools will allow us to do different effects to the landscape. And if we click on paint, we can use the paint tools to help us paint in various landscape layers that we've set up in a material for this particular landscape. Landscapes can also be utilized with blueprints as well as landscape splines which allow users to place down past that can even deform the landscape. Unreal Engine 5's landscape tools are very powerful and very easy to use. I highly encourage everyone to take a little time to mess around with these tools to unlock their true potential.
