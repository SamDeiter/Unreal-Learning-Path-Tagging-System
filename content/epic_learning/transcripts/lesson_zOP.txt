The purpose of this project is to demonstrate how to allow a user to teleport to preset locations around a map using a 3D UMG widget controlled by the motion controllers in VR. We will build this mechanic in two forms, top mode and preview mode. Top mode will consist of a top view of the scene with markers and places that we want to teleport to. A user simply points and clicks on the marker they want to travel to and off they go. Preview mode will animate the camera to the new locations point of view. The users can then cycle through the spots and press on the Travel to Location button and off they go. We will design these mechanics to be adaptable to which HMD hardware the end user is experiencing the project with. We'll also be keeping future updates in mind by thinking about how to develop the telepor system to scale with our needs. And speaking of scalability, an often requested mechanic that I receive from clients is the option to experience their scene in either VR or on PC. I want to quickly detail out what the flow of the project is so you have an end goal in mind while developing. After a user opens the game, they are presented with a start menu on their monitor with a single button to begin. When that button is pressed by the mouse, the HMD will become enabled and the game will begin. The starting pond will be destroyed and the VR pond will be spawned and possessed. The VR player can now spawn the 3D map in front of them by pressing and holding the trigger with both hands. As they hold the trigger, they control the location of the map in world space. The closer their hands are to each other, the further away the map will be from them. When they let go of a trigger, the map will stay in place and the user can point to the location or button in which they would like to travel to or interact with and press the trigger to interact.
