So what is Composure and how can it be used? The Composure plugin is currently a tool created for the high demand for real-time virtual production. Composure is filmed live action, integrated with Unreal Engine CG elements. So one of the many uses of Composure comes with the ability to create interactive content that can be adjusted in real time. Some examples of the adjustable features include the ability to change the CG actors albedo, roughness, and other material parameters. This course will focus on the basic real-time compositing workflow in Unreal Engine that includes bringing in pre-recorded footage or plates and creating an A over B composite with CG actors. However, it's important to note that Composure may also be used live or for broadcast if users have the correct equipment in place. Composure is moving away from traditional pipelines that most visual effects artists are used to in Nuke and After Effects, but the methodology is very similar and just presented in a different way. Composure is the ability of putting footage and CG together in real time.
