Thank you for being a part of this course on advanced PBR materials. Unreal Engine's material system is incredibly powerful and can be used to create high fidelity realistic materials and effects with relativ ease. In this course, we've covered a lot of ground. From some of the core tools available to us such as material Instance scene, material parameter collections, and material functions, to the use of shading models such as clear coat and cloth, we also looked at ways to use vertex colors to limit a material's effects to specific parts of a mesh, as well as using displacement mapping in unusual ways. Due to the near infinite variety of materials, there's so much more to learn. I hope though that this course, along with the previous introduction to materials course, gives you a good head start in knowing how to build your own advanced PBR materials. If you'd like to dig a little deeper into this topic, feel free to check out the Unreal Documentation on Materials, or the Unreal Engine forms rendering topic to get assistance. Another great option is to purchase material packs from the Unreal Marketplace. These packs provide lots of great example materials that you can use in your own projects, but they also provide a great way to learn from other members of the Unreal Engine community. This is Matthew Doyle for Epic Games and the Unreal Engine, wishing you the best with all your Unreal projects. Thanks for participating in the course.
