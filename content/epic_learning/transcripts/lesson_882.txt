Hi and welcome. My name is Shurt Young and welcome to this course on material essential concepts. We're going to go through all of the essentials of working with textures and material editor in the Unreal Engine. We will look at texture sizes and compression and how to import them. We will build our first material and talk through how to work with physical-based rendering within materials. And then once we have got that covered, we will dive deeper into the material editor itself by going through common expressions, material inputs, and other material properties such as different shading modes and blend modes. We will go through all of these topics by building small but fun examples. For instance, a material for a curtain moving in the wind or a rock material that blends in a grass texture at the top of a model it is applied to.
