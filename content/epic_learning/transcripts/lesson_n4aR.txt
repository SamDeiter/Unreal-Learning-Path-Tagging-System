To understand the differences, it's important to understand the difference between baked and dynamic lighting. Dynamic lights are able to move, rotate, and change intensity during runtime. We want to avoid having too many dynamic lights at any given time because these lights, and the shadows that they cast, take a lot of resources to render. Baked lights, on the other hand, are calculated prior to runtime. The system inside of Unreal Engine that calculates baked lighting is called Lightmass. Once baked lights are processed, lighting and shadow information is stored in your project, and the lights have a negligible impact on performance.
