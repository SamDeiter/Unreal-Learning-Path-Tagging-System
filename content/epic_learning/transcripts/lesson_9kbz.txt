Although there are no exercise files supplied with this course, the topics that we will be covering are designed to be applied to scenes as we go. And so the way to get the most out of this course would be to apply what is being taught to levels and projects of our own. As we go through the course material, there will be specific settings and even location values that I have to enter in order to make my particular scene look good. But you can simply transpose those parameters to your own scenes based on the information that you see presented.
