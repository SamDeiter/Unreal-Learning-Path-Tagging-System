One of the most important things to consider when creating a model for Unreal Engine is the Polygon Count. If your scene contains too many polygons on too many models, your frame rate will ultimately be lower than your target frame rate, in most cases 60 frames per second. In this module, we'll look at some things to consider as you model 3D geometry for an Unreal Engine project. The limit for how many polygons you can have rendered in your viewport at any given time is largely dependent on the hardware of the machine that will be rendering your Unreal Engine project. Broadly speaking, PCs can render millions of polygons while mobile platforms can render far less due to the system hardware. Typically, models should be created with as few polygons as they're needed. Extrusions and features that are part of the silhouette should always be modeled, but minor details should be added in the material instead of the geometry.
