With the official release of Unreal Engine 5 comes a broad set of new tools that we can learn and utilize in our projects to prepare them for next-generation hardware. Alongside these tools, we are also provided asset packs or full projects such as the city sample that we explored here. The city sample project includes asset packs such as the building asset pack, which is a collection of packed blueprints with collisions that also include some of the stuff we spoke about earlier, such as the materials on the glass that gave us what looked like an interior scene. Also included is the city sample vehicles pack and the crowds pack. Both can be placed directly into our projects or broken up to learn some tips on working with Nanite and Lumen, world partition, and how to fill the scene with crowds of people and or vehicles. I hope you are as excited as we are to take what has been built here and expand upon it. Some features that are here that we didn't have time to cover are the UI of the experience, the project's usage of MetaSounds, Niagara, AI, and the vehicle control rig among many others. There is so much here and we can't wait to see what you make from it. Happy exploring and thanks for watching.
