We will start by exploring the provided project download which includes a handful of simple but effective elements which we will use to develop virtually any typical studio setup. Though the project comes with a few simple studio blueprints, we will start by creating our own. Once we have developed a simple studio we are happy with, we will then move into IES profile lights which we can use to give our lighting a more dynamic and realistic quality. Next we will explore light panels and how to put them to use for both additional lighting and to hype up our reflections on the subject matter. Lastly we will explore the use of the rotator blueprint also included in the provided project download. This will give us the ability to rotate our cameras around the subject matter perfectly for look dev or for a dynamic presentation of your subject. Lastly we will learn how to use the cinematics tool to record and save our output. In the end you will be creating highly engaging studio lighting setups that are completely reusable. This tutorial concentrates on studio lighting for motorcycle. However the same techniques can be used for virtually any subject matter where you want to bathe your subject in reflections in light typical to a photographic studio. Also this tutorial series will take you far in regards to concept. The project conclusion however should be seen as a new starting point.
