Before we proceed with the course, be sure you have Unreal Engine 4.20 or better installed. You should already be comfortable using Unreal Engine and especially have a basic knowledge of creating and working with PBR materials. If you don't, you can go through my previous course on Introduction to Materials for Design Visualization or any of the other introductory material courses that are available. If you wish to view the source assets provided with this course, you'll need 3ds Max 2018 and V-Ray installed as well, but neither are required. You should have a good level of understanding materials in either a rendered setting or real-time workflow, preferably both. Being able to comfortably work in the Unreal Material Editor is also necessary for this course. Next up we'll take a look at the assets we'll be working with in the Unreal Editor.
