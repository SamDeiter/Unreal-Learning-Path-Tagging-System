A draw call is each aspect of a scene that requires processing bandwidth from your computer to be rendered in engine properly. A draw call contains a collection of information telling your computer and graphics card about textures, materials, and other aspects of assets of the scene that need to be rendered to your screen. With each additional draw call you have, the scene becomes more demanding to process and display at a proficient frame rate. As an artist, the term can seem a bit abstract, not knowing exactly what each draw call represents or is.
