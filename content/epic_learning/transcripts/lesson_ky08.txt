The Niagara Editor is Unreal Engine 5's tool for creating, modifying, and displaying visual effects. From inside of Niagara, you can do everything from create very simplistic 2D sprites to more realistic, fully dynamic 3D fluid simulations. Let's pop into the Editor and have a look at Niagara. So here's the Niagara Editor. As with many of our other editors across the top, we have our save browse, compile, thumbnail, and bounds, performance, debug simulation, Baker and scalability tools. These allow us to do things like view the rendering bounds, check on our performance, debug, any issues that we might have, as well as auto-play our simulation. The Baker allows us to actually bake out complex data to a more, less expensive, texture allowing us to still maintain this really complex look, but at a much cheaper version. And then scalability allows us to adjust how this is going to scale down on different systems. We then have our preview window, which allows us to view how our particle is going to look inside the scene. We then have our parameters, user parameters, and local modules.
