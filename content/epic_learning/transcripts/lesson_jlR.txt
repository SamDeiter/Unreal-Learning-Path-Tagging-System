When creating materials, we often rebuild the exact same functionality many times. This can become a tedious part of any workflow. Unreal offers a solution to this problem in the form of material functions. Material functions are simply custom-built, reusable snippets of material graphs, and these can be added to other materials across multiple projects. They have the added benefit of converting complicated material graphs into single material nodes with inputs and an output. Much like working with material instances, making changes to a material function means those changes will automatically propagate to all materials that make use of the custom code.
