Welcome to achieving epic results for your architectural, exterior, real-time projects. My name is Matthew Doyle and I'll be your instructor. I'm a technical artist and have worked in both the architectural and entertainment industries for nearly two decades, creating real-time interactive experiences in both real-world and fantasy environments, most often using the Unreal Engine. I also worked at Autodesk for seven years teaching and evangelizing 3DS Max, Maya, and Mudbox to content creators. This course will show you the power of Unreal Studio to create photorealistic architectural environments. I welcome you to follow me throughout the course, as we cover everything you'll need from start to finish to create a highly performant and visually stunning architectural exterior in Unreal Studio. The workflow will cover starts in 3DS Max with a completed architectural scene provided courtesy of TurboSquid. While the course uses 3DS Max assets, you can easily apply all the concepts to any other 3D tool. Most of the work will be done in Unreal Studio. In this case, we'll be using version 4.19. We'll start off by looking at how to prepare your assets for export into Unreal Studio and examine some ways to optimize your content for the best performance. After that, we'll work through the process of adding terrain, foliage, and other effects that improve the viewer's immersion in the environment. Then we'll cover the requirements to creating photorealistic lighting and using post-processing effects such as color grading, as well as camera lens effects, such as light bloom and lens flare. We'll also look at how to add interactivity to your scene, allowing the viewer to move around. Last of all, we'll go over debugging and testing options available to you, ending with how to distribute your walkthroughs. Once we're finished, we'll have a completed photorealistic and interactive real-time walkthrough that can be run on any modern computer.
