What is material, why are they used, and how would we create our own basic materials? So what is a material? Materials define the surface properties of the objects in our scene. You can think of a material as the paint that's applied to a mesh to control its visual appearance. In more technical terms, they tell the render engine exactly how a surface should interact with the light in your scene, or even if it should interact with the light. They define every aspect of a surface, including color, reflectivity, bumpiness, transparency, and so on. These calculations are done using data that is input into the material from a variety of sources such as images, colors, or even math nodes.
