So first thing, what is Nanite? Nanite is Unreal Engine 5's new virtualized geometry system. It uses a new internal mesh format and rendering technology to render pixel-scale details and high object counts. It intelligently does work on only the detail that can be perceived and no more. Nanite's data format is also highly compressed and supports fine-grained streaming with automatic level of detail. So what would we use Nanite 4 and why? Nanite is going to give you multiple orders of magnitude increase in geometry complexity, along with higher triangle and objects count. Your frame budgets are no longer constrained by polycounts, draw calls, or mesh memory usage. It's now possible to directly import film quality source arts, such as from ZBrush or Photogrammetry Scans. We can now use high poly detailing rather than baking detail into normal map textures, which save us both size, on disk and memory, and time. Level of detail or lads are automatically handled and no longer require manual setup for each individual mesh. Along with this, the loss of quality is rare or even non-existent, especially when you're doing LOD transitions.
