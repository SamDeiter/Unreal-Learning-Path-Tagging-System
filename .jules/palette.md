## 2025-07-18 - Accessibility popovers and settings controls
**Learning:** Raw emojis (⚙️, ×, 🔊, ⏸️, ▶️) inside buttons lack visual styling control and can behave unpredictably on different platforms/browsers, failing visual and screen-reader accessibility standards. Additionally, modal dialogs must trap keyboard focus (`Tab`/`Shift+Tab`), focus an element when opened, and restore focus to the trigger button when closed to prevent screen-reader disorientation.
**Action:** Always replace emojis/ASCII characters with equivalent `lucide-react` icons (e.g. `Settings`, `X`, `Volume2`, `Pause`, `Play`) styled with explicit sizes and `aria-hidden="true"`. Implement ref-based focus restoration, auto-focus, and focus-trapping `keydown` listeners on all interactive modal or popover containers.
