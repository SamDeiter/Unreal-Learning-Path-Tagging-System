## 2025-05-14 - Individual History Deletion in Search Interfaces
**Learning:** Users value the ability to curate their local history, especially in diagnostic tools where queries can be personal or experimental. Providing a delete mechanism on recent items improves perceived privacy and interface cleanliness.
**Action:** When implementing recent query history, always provide an individual item removal mechanism (e.g., a "×" button) with clear ARIA labels and title tooltips for accessibility.
